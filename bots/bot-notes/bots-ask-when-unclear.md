New: Bots ask when an order is unclear
When an order could mean more than one person, Ultimus, the Seven and True Form now ask a short question instead of guessing. "Blade" or "heal him" in a group of three gets "Blade who: you, me or Walter?", and your answer, like "me" or "Walter", finishes the order. A word that is two letters off, like "shleid me", gets "Did you mean shield me?" and a "yes" carries it out.
Where: In the game, in a group you lead with one of these bots.
Test: In a battle with two other players on your side, type "blade" and answer the bot's question with a name.

New: Bots remember the last order
"Again" or "same" repeats your last order, and "Walter too" or "and Mindy" puts your last order on that person as well. Out of battle, "kill the outlaw" or "kill them" with an enemy right next to you now starts the fight instead of waiting for the next one.
Where: In the game, in a group you lead with one of these bots.
Test: Type "blade me", then "Walter too", and the bot should blade Walter as well.
