Fixed: True Form has a full healing setup and a full tank setup

You can now ask True Form for "full healing setup" or "full tank setup", like "go full healer for me". He answers with the schools to pick from, best first: Life, Death and Balance for healing, Ice, Balance and Death for tanking. Say the school, like "life", and he switches. You can also name it at once, like "go life full heal". The healing setup puts every piece of gear, jewel and pet talent on outgoing healing and he plays support. The tank setup puts everything on resist and he plays defend. If you pick a school that is not the best fit, he still does it and tells you which would be better. He goes back to his normal setup when the group ends.

Where: chatting with True Form, in a group or before grouping.
Test: Group with True Form and say "full healing setup", check he lists Life first, then say "life" and check his gear and jewels show outgoing healing when he is back. Then say "go ice full tank" and check his resist is high.
