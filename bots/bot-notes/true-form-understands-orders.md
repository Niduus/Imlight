Fixed: True Form understands orders said many ways
True Form now takes off his name, "hey", "please" and "can you" before reading an order, so "True Form, can you please heal us?" is understood as support. He also gets small typos like "atack" or "suport". Anyone in his group can order him in normal chat without saying his name. He now answers "what mode are you in", "use" works as "cast" or "keep" too, and "stop using spells" lets him pick his own cards again. A line like "go back to the door" or "that boss hits hard" no longer sends him home or changes how he plays.
Where: In the game, in a group with True Form.
Test: Group with him and say "can you defend please", "atack", "cast dragon blade", "what mode are you in" and "you can leave".
