Fixed: True Form picks his jewels the way players do

True Form used to fill his sockets with flat damage and flat resist jewels, and missed the newest jewels such as the +155 health tear. Now he finds every jewel in the game's files and picks the way players do. Circles get percent damage or pierce, whichever raises his hits more with what he wears. Triangles get accuracy until his spells hit 100 percent, then power pip chance until 100 percent. Squares get percent damage or resist, and for Death and Life wizards outgoing healing counts too. Flat damage is never picked, and flat resist only when it is resist to every school.

Where: True Form's gear, after a server restart. The first start reads the game's files once more in the background, so his jewels can take a few minutes to change.
Test: Restart, wait a few minutes, then look at True Form's gear up close. His tear sockets should hold +155 health, his triangles accuracy or power pip jewels, his circles damage or pierce, and none of his jewels should be flat damage.
