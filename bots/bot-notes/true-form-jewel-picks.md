Fixed: True Form picks percent damage jewels over flat ones

True Form used to fill his sockets with flat damage and flat resist jewels, which players rarely want. Now flat damage jewels are never picked, and flat resist only counts when it is resist to every school, well below a percent damage jewel. His circle sockets take a percent damage or a pierce jewel, whichever raises his hits more with the gear he wears, so he takes pierce while his pierce is low and damage once he has plenty.

Where: True Form's gear and pet, after a server restart.
Test: Restart, look at True Form's gear up close and check that his sockets hold percent damage jewels, pierce jewels in some circles, and no flat damage jewels.
