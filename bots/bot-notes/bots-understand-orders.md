Fixed: Ultimus, the Seven and True Form understand their group leader better
Orders are now read the way people really type them. Small typos like "sheild me" or "folow me" work, polite asks like "can you blade Mindy next round?" are taken as orders instead of questions, and "you will blade me" or "Garm, heal me" keep who it is for. "Kill them" or "kill the mobs" now means hit the enemies, a card named on its own like "Legion Shield us" is cast, and "go find Walter" sends them to that group mate. Lines like "kill yourself" are never taken as an order.
Where: In the game, in a group you lead with Ultimus, one of the Seven or True Form.
Test: Lead a group with one of them and type "sheild me", "can you heal me?" and "kill them" in a battle.

Fixed: Bots use the right card for who the order is for
"Blade me" or "heal Mitsu" now picks a card that can go on that teammate instead of one that only works on the bot itself, and "kill them all" picks a spell that hits every enemy. True Form is dealt the card he was told to use right away, so "blade me" no longer ends with "I don't have a blade card".
Where: In the game, in a battle with a bot in your group.
Test: In a battle with True Form, type "blade me" and watch him blade you that round or the next.

Fixed: Bots say what they understood, and why they can't
In battle a bot now answers with what it understood, like "Okay, I'll blade you!", instead of a plain "Okay!". When it then can't do the order, like a name that isn't in the battle, it now says so instead of staying quiet.
Where: In the game, in a battle with a bot in your group.
Test: In a battle, tell a bot "blade Nobody" and it should say it can't see them.
