Fixed: True Form answers his commands and keeps the stronger aura
Typing .trueform on its own showed a list of commands instead of calling him, and a call made while he was in a battle often ran out before he saw it. Now .trueform calls him right away, and every .trueform word (attack, defend, support, auto, evil, use, leave, where) works the same way, in a battle too. In battle he no longer puts a weaker aura or global over a stronger one already up, like a 100% aura for 4 rounds over a 666% one with 66 rounds left; he keeps what is up and plays something else or passes.
Where: True Form's commands and chat, and his battles.
Test: Type .trueform near him, then .trueform defend and .trueform where. In a battle with a strong aura on him, check he does not cast a weaker one over it.
