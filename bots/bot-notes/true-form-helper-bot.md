New: True Form, a helper bot anyone can call
True Form (his real name is Ahziael) is a top level Myth wizard in the best Myth gear the game has, with every spell in the game, one of each, treasure cards and upgraded spells included. When nobody has him he waits at his spot in The Commons by the carousel. Type .trueform or say "True Form, join me" and he sends you a group invite, hands you the lead, comes to you and joins your battles, using only spells of your level, plus dev spells that never one-shot. He picks attack, defend or support each round from how the fight goes, or plays the way you ask (.trueform attack, defend, support, auto, or evil for a troll who hampers his own team on purpose); .trueform use Dragonblade keeps a spell in his hand, .trueform leave sends him home and .trueform where says where he is.
Where: The Commons by the carousel, and in any group with him. Turned on or off in Imlight.ini under [TrueForm].
Test: Type .trueform near him, accept the invite, fight a few battles at your level and try .trueform defend, .trueform evil and .trueform leave. A QA account can stand on the exact spot and type .trueform home to set his spot.
