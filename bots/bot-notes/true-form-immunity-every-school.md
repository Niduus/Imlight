Fixed: Universal Immunity aims for 100% resist to every school

Immunity to a school means 100% or more resist to it, counting that school's own resist and every-school resist. True Form's Universal Immunity setup used to count only every-school resist. Now it picks the gear and set that make him immune to the most schools, then brings the rest as close to 100% as it can. After he changes, he whispers you which schools his gear makes him immune to and which still fall short.
Where: True Form, "universal immunity" setup.
Test: Tell True Form "go ice universal immunity" and read his whisper about which schools reached 100%.
