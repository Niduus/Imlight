New: Tell True Form to switch school

In a group, tell True Form "go ice for me" and he switches to Ice with the setup that school does best. Ice tanks, Life heals, Fire and Storm hit, and Myth, Death and Balance hit and help the team. Add a role, like "go ice and tank for me" or "go fire and heal me", and he plays that role. If the role does not suit the school, such as Storm tanking or Fire healing, he still tries but warns you first. To switch, he steps out for a moment, changes into that school's best level 190 gear, jewels, pet and deck, and comes back to your group. His stitched look stays the same in every school, and each school's gear and pet stay in his backpack for next time. When he leaves the group he goes back to Myth.

Where: True Form, in a group.
Test: Group with True Form and say "True Form, go ice for me". Check that he comes back as Ice with Ice gear and pet and the same look. Then say "go storm and tank for me" and check that he warns you. Leave the group and check that he is Myth again.
