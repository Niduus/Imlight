Fixed: True Form offers all his pets at the Pet Hatching Kiosk

True Form now puts every pet he has up at the Pet Hatching Kiosk, so anyone can hatch with them there, even while he is busy or offline. Each time he changes setup, his listings are refreshed with his pets' current talents, and any pet he no longer has comes off the kiosk. Eggs and pets below the server's hatching level stay off.
Where: any Pet Hatching Kiosk, under the pets other wizards have offered.
Test: Open a Pet Hatching Kiosk, look through the offered pets, find True Form's, and hatch one of your pets with it.
