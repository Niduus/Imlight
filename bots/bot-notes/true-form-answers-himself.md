Fixed: True Form answers questions about himself properly

True Form gave wrong answers to some questions. "What is in your deck?" listed his fishing spells, "What all options do you have?" got the questing bot's answer, and questions about his treasure cards got vague guesses or made him draw a card. Now he answers from what he really has: his spells and deck, his treasure cards, his school, and a short list of what you can tell him to do. "Do you need healing?" now gets his health back instead of switching him to support. Asking him to change school before you group, like "become fire please", makes him come and switch once you group, and asking for Moon or another astral school gets a friendly no.

Where: chatting with True Form, in a group or not.
Test: Ask True Form "what is in your deck?", "what treasure cards do you have?", "what is your school?", "what all options do you have?" and "do you need healing?". Then, before grouping, whisper "become fire please" and check that he invites you and switches to Fire once you group.
