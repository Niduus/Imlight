Fixed: True Form's pet matches his school, and his school survives bot updates

When True Form switched to another school, his pet could still carry Myth talents, like the Ice owl showing Myth Dealer and Myth Giver. Now his pet takes the talents of the school he is playing, with every pet stat at 260. A bot update or a server restart used to drop him back to Myth in the middle of your group; now he comes back as the school you asked for and invites you again, and only goes back to Myth if you do not take him back within a few minutes. When his gear already gives him enough accuracy or power pips, his jewels and pet now go for universal accuracy instead, so his spells from other schools hit too.

Where: True Form's pet, jewels and school switching.
Test: Group with True Form and say "go ice for me", then look at his pet's talents and check they show Ice. While still grouped, wait for a bot update or restart the bots, and check he comes back as Ice and invites you.
