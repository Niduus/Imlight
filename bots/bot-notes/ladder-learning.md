New: Ultimus, the TCO7 and the Kitharas Family learn from each other
Every questing bot now reads what all the others did on the same quest. A bot that starts a quest is told the fastest time any bot has done it in, and tries to beat that instead of only Ultimus's time. When it gets stuck or starts the quest, it also hears what the bots who finished it learned there, and what went wrong for any of them so it does not repeat it. Fights work the same way: each bot now picks its fight style from the results of every bot added together, so a lesson one of them paid for in lost fights helps all of them. Ultimus learns from the TCO7 and the Kitharas Family too, not only the other way round. Two new files in the Ultimus logs folder sum it up: ladder-quests.txt and ladder-fights.txt.
Where: Every quest in the Main Story, and every fight they play.
Test: Let the TCO7 play a while, then open logs\ultimus\ladder-quests.txt and check it lists quests with each bot's time, what worked and what to avoid. In a quest log, a STARTED line now names the bot whose time is the one to beat.
