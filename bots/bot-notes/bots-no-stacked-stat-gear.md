Fixed: Bots no longer wear gear with stacked fake stats

True Form wore "Valiant Jouster's Sabatons", boots with no level that give +1198 max health five times over. No player can get them. Any such piece a bot already wears comes off at once.
Where: Every bot's gear, True Form most of all.
