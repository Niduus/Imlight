New: Ultimus checks every "click these things" quest step after an update

After each update, Ultimus checks every main story step that asks you to click several things, like the gems in "Get Some Bling". He writes the list to use-steps.txt in his logs folder. Steps whose things he can find right away are listed as placed. Steps where the things only appear later are listed at the top, because those are where he may need to search.

Where: Ultimus, use-steps.txt in his logs folder.
Test: Update, let Ultimus start, then open use-steps.txt. The top part lists the steps worth a look.
