Fixed: Ultimus's new rules can be switched off or set to watch only, and repeated lines no longer flood his log
Each new Ultimus rule now has a line in the Rules part of his playbook: 2 means on, 1 means watch only, and 0 means off. In watch-only mode he plays as before and only writes what he would have done in his Thoughts and in shadow-log.txt, so a new rule can be tried for a night before it takes control, and a rule that turns out wrong can be switched off at once without an update. The rules so far are FlipFlop, SideTripDrop, OfferedDoor and RepeatGuard. The repeat guard holds back a line he says more than 6 times in a minute and shows one pink REPEAT line instead; the count is saved in repeats.txt. The potion trip that marked his spot twice a second is also fixed: the mark is now kept until he has left the area.
Where: Ultimus playbook (playbook.txt), Rules section; files shadow-log.txt and repeats.txt in his logs folder.
Test: Set Rules.SideTripDrop = 1 in his playbook and save: within a few seconds the rule only writes "WATCH ONLY" lines. Set it back to 2 afterwards.
