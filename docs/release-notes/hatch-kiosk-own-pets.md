Fixed: The Pet Hatching Kiosk is full of pets, and younger pets can go up
The kiosk now has pets of its own of every kind in the game, about six thousand, made from your game files the first time someone uses a kiosk and kept in pet.kiosk next to the server. Some look like a player just leveled them to Mega without caring about talents, and others are built for one job, like damage for one, two or three schools, critical, accuracy, pierce, resist, healing, pips or selfish stats, so Search Pet Talents finds pets for any talent you pick. A new Kiosk setting in Ahziael picks the youngest age a player's pet can be to go up, and because the game's Offer Pets window only lists Adult and older pets, a younger pet goes up by typing .kiosk and its name.
Where: Ahziael, Server, Pet Hatching Kiosk bot (Youngest pets allowed and The kiosk's own pets), and any Pet Hatching Kiosk in game.
Test: Open a kiosk, browse by type and search a few talents, then set Youngest pets allowed to Baby and type .kiosk with a Baby pet's name.
