New: Ultimus notices when he goes in circles
If Ultimus keeps coming back to the same area, or keeps picking the same move, four times in 20 minutes without moving his story on, he now calls it a loop. He stops, sets that move aside for a while, forgets the ways he tried and makes a new plan. The loop is written in his mistakes book and shown on the Ultimus Issues page.
Where: Ultimus, no setting.
Test: Watch the Ultimus log for a line starting with LOOP. After it, he should do something different from what he was repeating.
