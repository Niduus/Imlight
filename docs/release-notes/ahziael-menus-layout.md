Fixed: The side bar's lowest rows can be clicked again
On smaller screens, or with Windows text made bigger, Ahziael opened taller than the screen, so the bottom of the side bar sat under the taskbar and its last rows could not be reached. Ahziael now opens no bigger than your screen, the rows have more room between them, and the list scrolls clear past its last row with the scroll bar always in sight.
Where: Help > Guides
Test: Open Ahziael on a laptop screen, open the Help page in the side bar, and click Credits at the very bottom.

Changed: All the bots are on one page
Ultimus, the TCO7, the Quest Team and the street Bot Players now sit together on one Bots page in the side bar. Each one is a part you click to open its own tabs. The Bot Players switch and settings moved from Battles and World to their own Street Bots tab there.
Where: Bots > Street Bots
Test: Open Bots in the side bar, click TCO7 to see its members, then click Street bots and turn Bot Players on or off.

Changed: Pages use the whole window
On a wide window, pages made of cards now show them in two columns instead of one narrow column in the middle. Lists such as Commands, Quests and Phrases use the full width too. A window of the usual size looks as before.
Where: Bots > Ultimus
Test: Make Ahziael full screen on a wide monitor and open the Ultimus tab or Settings; the cards should sit side by side.

New: Lock a zone right where you find it
Each zone now has Teleport, Lock this zone and Who can teleport into its rooms together, both on the Locations tab and when you search for it. Lock is the same switch as On and Off and never teleports you. The search window now calls these Zones.
Where: Database > Locations
Test: Search Unicorn Way, press Lock this zone, then try to go there in the game; press Unlock this zone to open it again.

New: The bots' treasure card stock with counts
Each bot's sheet now shows every treasure card it owns, in its deck or not, with how many of each, as card pictures with the number on them and as a line of names.
Where: Bots > Ultimus
Test: With Ultimus playing, open his page and look under his deck for Treasure Card stock.

Changed: Testing tools removed or marked
Quest Testing, Boss Test and Shop research were tools made for testing Imlight while it was built, and they confused players, so they are gone from Ahziael. Team Issues and Ultimus Issues stay, with a note at the top that they are about the bots' own testing.
Where: Bots > Ultimus Issues
Test: Look in the side bar under Quests and Create; Quest Testing and Boss Test are no longer there. Open Ultimus Issues and see the yellow note at the top.
