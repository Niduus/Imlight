Fixed: Another wizard's pet card shows its current talents

When you looked at another wizard's pet, the game kept showing its old copy if the pet's level and stats had not changed, even after its talents or the pet itself were changed. The server now always sends the pet's current details, so the pet card is always up to date.

Where: Character window of another wizard, pet card.
Test: Look at True Form's pet, have him switch setup, then look again and check the talents changed.
