New: See where each stat comes from
Hovering a stat on a wizard's Stats card in Ahziael now lists the worn pieces that give to it and how much each gives, like "Robe name: +12%". Whatever is left over comes from the wizard's level, pet, jewels and other effects, and is shown on one last line. The same Stats card is used for Ultimus, the Quest Team and Bot Players, so it works on all three.
Where: Ahziael, the Stats card on the Ultimus, Quest Team and Bot Players pages.
Test: Open Ultimus's page and hover "Balance damage": the pieces adding damage are listed with their numbers.
