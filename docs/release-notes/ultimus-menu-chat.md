# Ultimus talks in Menu Chat

New: Ultimus now talks to players, using Menu Chat only (the game's own menu phrases), and only now and then.
- When a player walks into its battle, it may say a welcome ("Welcome", "Hello").
- When it walks into a player's battle for its quest, it may say "I'll help" or similar.
- After a battle won together, it may say "Good job".
- If a player says "help", "join" or "fight" while Ultimus is fighting, it says yes ("Sure").
- If a player asks it to follow, trade, group, teleport or wait, it says no ("No thanks"): its story quest comes first.
- It says hello, thanks and bye back.
It never talks with the Quest Team, and the Quest Team never answers it.

Where: in game, near Ultimus. Every line it says or hears is written in Ahziael > Ultimus > Ultimus Logs.

Test:
1. Turn Ultimus on in Ahziael and go to where it is (Ultimus > TP to Ultimus).
2. Say "Hello" in Menu Chat next to it: it says hello back.
3. Say "Follow me": it turns it down.
4. Walk into its battle: it may welcome you in Menu Chat.
