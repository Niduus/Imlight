Fixed: Two players boarding a flight or ship together get split up
When two players took the Ironworks flight together, the second one to board threw away the ride the first one was still loading into. The first player ended up in an empty zone, looked offline to the other, and the pair landed in different copies of the dungeon. Now a team mate boarding with you always joins your ride, on every flight and ship ride in every world.
Where: Any ride a dungeon pad starts, like the Marleybone flights to the Ironworks and the Krokotopia ships.
Test: Stand on the Ironworks pad with a friend and board together. You both ride the same flight, land in the same Ironworks, and see each other online the whole time.
