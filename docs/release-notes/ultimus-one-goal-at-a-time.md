Fixed: Ultimus no longer walks back and forth between two places
When two of Ultimus's own rules kept sending him two different ways, he could walk between two areas for an hour, as he did between The Ironworks and Nail's Hideout. Now he notices when two goals keep taking turns with nothing getting done, picks one (the one that opens the way for the other, or the quest step over a side trip), and finishes it before the other. If that does not help, he tries the other one, and then asks for help on the Ultimus Issues page. Walking into an area he was just in no longer counts as progress, so his loop alarms now act instead of only counting. Every case is saved in detail in flip-flops.jsonl, which Copy all logs includes, and it can be switched off in his playbook with Guard.FlipFlopBreaker = 0.
Where: Ultimus, on by default; playbook rules Guard.FlipFlopBreaker, Guard.FlipFlopSwitches, Guard.FlipFlopWindowMinutes and Guard.FlipFlopMinutes.
Test: Let Ultimus play a dungeon with its own quest, like Gate Crashers in The Ironworks. He should not bounce between two rooms; if he starts to, the Ultimus log shows a pink FLIP-FLOP line and he keeps to one goal.
