Fixed: Bots show up in the Friendly Players list
The Friendly Players window (your friends list) now lists every bot that is on: Ultimus, the TCO7, True Form, the Quest Team and the Kitharas Family. Each one shows its level, school and where it is, the same as a friend, and comes off the list when it logs off. You can open a bot's page or go to it from the list like any friend. Nothing is saved, so if you remove a bot from the list it stays gone until you log in again. The walking street Bot Players are not listed yet.
Where: In game, the Friendly Players window. No setting.
Test: Turn on Ultimus or the TCO7, log in and open the Friendly Players window. The bots that are on should be listed with their level and place. Turn one off and within about 10 seconds it should leave the list.
