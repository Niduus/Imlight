Fixed: Players are warned before the bots go down for an update

When a bot update came in, every bot logged out and back in with no warning. Now everyone online gets a chat line before the bots go down, then at 1 minute and 30 seconds, then when they go down and when they are back, just like the server's own restart for an update. Nothing holds the bots back: battles and players do not delay them, and with nobody online they update at once. The warning can be turned off and its length changed (2 minutes to start) on Ahziael's Bot Updates part, and changes count at once.

Where: game chat, whenever a bot update arrives; Ahziael, Bots page, Bot updates part.
Test: Set the warning time to 1 minute on the Bot Updates part, stay logged in when a bot update arrives and watch chat for the warnings and the back line. Then turn Warn players first off and check the next bot update comes with no warning.
