Fixed: The Watering Hole in Off the Boil can be used again

Savannah's Watering Hole sits in the middle of a dry basin you can't walk into, and its nearest edge is a long way from it. The server only offered things like it from close to their nearest floor, so nobody standing on the edge was ever offered "Use" and the quest could not be finished. Now the server looks farther for the nearest floor around such quest things and offers them from there, which also covers any other quest thing that stands this far from the ground.
Where: Zafaria, Savannah, the quest Off the Boil (step "Restore Watering Hole").
Test: Take Off the Boil to the Restore Watering Hole step, walk to the edge of the dry basin nearest the Watering Hole, and check that you are offered "Use" and the step finishes.
