Fixed: Mirage cave by the Caravan warped players back to where they stood
In the first area of Mirage, the cave teleports that take you up and down the big rock had their landing spots swapped. Walking into the cave at the bottom put you back at the bottom, and the one at the top put you back at the top, so it looked like the cave only warped you to itself. Each one now takes you to the other end, like in the real game.
Where: Mirage, the Caravan area (first area of Mirage), the cave teleports up and down the rock.
Test: In Mirage's first area, walk into the cave at the bottom of the rock: you should come out on top. Walk into the one on top: you should come out at the bottom.
