New: Ultimus Spectator overlay add-on
Watch Ultimus while you play. He gets his own minimap that follows him wherever he is and always draws HIS quest's way, never yours, next to a box with his quest, step, health and where he is. In battle a spells box shows his hand (the card he picks lit up, cards he can't cast yet faded), his pips, what he casts and why, what he threw away and the enemies' health. It needs the Minimap add-on (it comes with it when you download it), and your own Minimap and Quest Tracker stay exactly as they are. The host can turn it off with UltimusSpectator under [Overlay]; it applies right away.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Turn Ultimus on, then in Play > Overlay > Add-ons download "Ultimus Spectator" from Niduus's add-ons and tick "Ultimus Spectator (map, quest and spells)". Start the game: the purple map on the left follows Ultimus with his quest line, and when he fights, his spells box shows up next to it.

New: Add-ons can need other add-ons
An add-on can now say which other add-ons it needs. Downloading it also downloads those, and ticking its pieces puts the needed add-on on your overlay too when it isn't there yet.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Remove the Minimap add-on, download Ultimus Spectator, and check that the Minimap came with it.
