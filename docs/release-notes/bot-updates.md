New: Bots update on their own
The bots (Ultimus, the Essentials, the Quest Team and the street bots) now get their fixes from a separate bot update, so Ahziael no longer needs a full update just for bot changes. When a new bot update is published, every server picks it up within about ten minutes, waits until no bot is in a battle, and restarts only the bots while players stay online. Bot fixes now have their own notes, so Ahziael's patch notes stay shorter.
Where: Ultimus > Bot Updates
Test: Open Bot Updates and check that the switch is on and the page says which bots are running. After a bot update is published, press Check now and watch the bots log out and come back on their own.
