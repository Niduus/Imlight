New: Optional AI chat for Ultimus on your own PC
Ultimus can use a language model running on your PC (Ollama or LM Studio) to chat more naturally and follow a conversation. It is given only his real facts (level, area, quest, step, health, latest memories) and the talk with that player. Private details stay blocked whatever the AI says, it is never used for anything that makes him act, and his normal chat answers whenever the AI is off, slow or not running. Nothing goes to the internet. Off by default; changes count right away.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus, the new Ultimus AI Chat page. In Imlight.ini: [Ultimus] LocalAI, LocalAIUrl, LocalAIModel.
Test: Follow the Ollama steps on the page, click Test connection, turn AI chat on, then whisper Ultimus "What's your favorite school?" and "what about ice?".

New: Model picks with or without a scan
The AI Chat page has a table of models by PC strength to pick from yourself, and a "Scan my PC for a recommendation" button. Only when you press it, Ahziael reads this PC's memory and graphics card and recommends an app and a model. Nothing from the scan is sent anywhere. It also has click-by-click setup steps for Ollama and LM Studio, and plain help when the test fails.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus, Ultimus AI Chat.
Test: Press Scan my PC for a recommendation: it names your memory, graphics card and a model to download.
