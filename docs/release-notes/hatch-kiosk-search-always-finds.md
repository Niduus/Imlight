Fixed: Search Pet Talents always finds pets

When a talent search at the Pet Hatching Kiosk finds fewer than three pets, the kiosk now makes new pets that show the talents you picked, so the list is never empty. A pet shows at most five talents, so when you search for more than five with "Search for all talents", the new pets show the first five you picked and the game tells you so. The new pets stay at the kiosk from then on.

Where: Pet Hatching Kiosk, Search Pet Talents.
Test: Search for a rare mix of three to five talents with "Search for all talents" and check at least three pets come up, each showing all of them; then pick seven talents and check the pets show the first five.
