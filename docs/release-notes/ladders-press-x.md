Fixed: Ladders and zip-lines offer their press X
Ladders and zip-lines never showed their press X, so you could not climb or ride them. They now work like doors: walk up to one, press X, and you come out at the other end.
Where: every world with ladders or zip-lines, such as Tyrian Gorge and The Hive in Khrysalis.
Test: walk up to a ladder in Tyrian Gorge, press X, and check that you land at the other end and not inside the cliff.
