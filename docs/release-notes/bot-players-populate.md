New: Bots fill the streets
With Bot Players on, every street with real players in it gets bots: each player brings 3 to 6, one street has at most 18 and the Commons at most 12. They walk in one by one from elsewhere in the street, and leave when the players do. Dungeons, houses, guild areas and the arena never get them.
Where: Game Rules > Battles and World
Test: Turn on Bot Players and Fill the streets, go to a street and wait a minute: bots should come in until there are 3 to 6 for you.

Changed: Bots look like their own wizards
Every bot has its own gender, face, hair and colors from the character maker, and wears the title of a badge a wizard of its level has likely earned.
Where: Game Rules > Battles and World
Test: Look at a few bots in a street: their faces and hair should differ, and each has a badge title over its name.

Changed: Bots walk like people
A bot wanders between places in its street in no set order, along a slight curve and at a pace that changes a little. At the end of a walk it goes on, stops and looks around, or stands a while.
Where: Game Rules > Battles and World
Test: Watch a bot for a minute.

Changed: A bot picks in circle order
In a battle a bot waits only for the real players on the circles before its own, then picks, and picks again when a player after it picks or changes their card.
Where: Game Rules > Battles and World
Test: With a friend, stand so the bot's circle is between yours; the bot picks after the first player and changes its pick to fit the second.
