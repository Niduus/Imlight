Fixed: Marleybone airship flights never landed by themselves
The blimps from the Marleybone airport to Chelsea Court and the Ironworks, and the ones back, flew and then left you on board when the trip ended, until you clicked the sign. Now the end of each flight takes you where that sign goes, and every boarding gets a fresh blimp of your own.
Where: Marleybone airport, flights to and from Chelsea Court and the Ironworks.
Test: board a flight, do not click anything, and check that you land at the other end after about 35 seconds.
