Fixed: The Ultimus Spectator has a Pause AI button
The Ultimus Spectator add-on now comes with a "Pause AI (1 hour)" button above his minimap. Clicking it pauses Ultimus's AI for an hour, and clicking it again turns the AI back on. It only works on the PC that runs the server.
Where: Ahziael > Overlay > add-ons > Ultimus Spectator
Test: Put the Ultimus Spectator on the overlay, click "Pause AI (1 hour)" in the game, and check that the top of the Ultimus page says "paused until".
