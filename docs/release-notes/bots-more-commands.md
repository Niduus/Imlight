Added: More in-game commands for Ultimus, the TCO7 and the Quest Team
You can now ask a bot what it is doing and why, pause or resume it, have it explain its plan, send it back to its story, and see the watchdog book, all from the game. A name picks the bot: none means Ultimus, a TCO7 name picks that member, and all or tco7 picks several at once. The Quest Team gets why, pause and resume too.
Where: In the game, .ultimus help lists them all (why, pause, resume, explain, story, squad, watchdog) and .questteam why, pause and resume.
Test: Type .ultimus why all and check that each bot says what it is doing, then .ultimus pause Tulkar and .ultimus resume Tulkar and check that Tulkar stops and goes on.
