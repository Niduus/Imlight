Fixed: Too many tough street creatures joining one fight
Out on a shared street, creatures ranked as bosses that wander in a group could all walk into one fight, filling every circle against a single wizard. They now follow the battle size rule like any other creature, so one wizard faces two of them, two wizards face three, and so on. Bosses inside dungeons and other private copies still join their own boss fight as before.
Where: MooShu, Tatakai Outpost, the Ronin Keyholders of "Key to Success" (and any street with boss-ranked creatures).
Test: Start a fight with a Ronin Keyholder alone and watch that no more than two Keyholders end up in the battle.
