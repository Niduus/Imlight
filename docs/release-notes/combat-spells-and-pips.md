Fixed: Enemies no longer spam their Natural Attack
Enemy creatures used their Natural Attack round after round again. Now an enemy has to wait one round after a Natural Attack before it can use it again, so it casts its other spells in between.
Test: Fight a creature that has a Natural Attack (Skeletal Pirates are a good one) and check it never uses it two rounds in a row.

Changed: Creatures use X pip spells more wisely
Creatures used to throw spells like Tempest and Judgement with only one or two pips. Now they cast another attack first when they have one, and with only X pip spells they sometimes keep their pips a round so the spell hits harder.
Test: Fight a creature with an X pip spell and check it waits until it has about four pips before casting it.

Fixed: Power pips work for Sun, Moon, Star and Shadow spells
A wizard who took Sun, Moon, Star or Shadow as their astral school could not use power pips as 2 pips for that school's spells. Now power pips count double for them, like for their own school's spells.
Test: Take an astral school with .astral, then in a battle with power pips check that its spells can be cast.

Fixed: School pips not coming and the Power Orb showing no school
In some fights the Power Orb by a wizard's name showed no school icon and school pips never came. The orb now always falls back to the wizard's own school when no proper school is set for it.
Test: Fight with a wizard of level 50 or more and check the orb by the name shows the school and school pips come.

Fixed: Prism, Sharpened Blade and Potent Trap stacking
A sharpened blade or a potent trap did not stack with a plain one of the same kind, as it does in the game. A prism also skipped the traps and shields put on before it. Now they stack, and a prism changes the hit where it stands, so the bubbles and the damage match what the game shows.
Test: Put a Potent Trap and a plain trap of the same school on a creature and hit it, then check both bubbles go and the damage counts both.
