New: Ahziael tells you in red when a door didn't take Ultimus or the Quest Team

When a walk-through door still doesn't take Ultimus or the Quest Team after a full round of tries, the Ultimus page in Ahziael now shows a red note at the top. Press Copy the door tries and paste it in the chat, and the door can be fixed from that alone. Got it hides the note until the next door fails.

Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page, top box.
Test: The note only shows after a door really fails. Open the Ultimus page and check that no red note shows while every door works.
