New: Grid and snapping in the overlay designer
The designer picture (and the designer on the game) has a grid. You can show or hide it and set the square size, how often a stronger line shows, its colour and how see-through it is. While you drag, a piece snaps to the window's edges and middle, to other pieces' edges and middles (a guide line shows which), or else to the grid. Hold Alt to drag freely. Arrow keys move the picked piece one pixel at a time, or one grid square with Shift. The grid only shows while designing, never on the game.
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it > Grid and snapping (on the game: the Grid and Snap ticks in the bar)
Test: Turn on the grid, set the square size to 40, and drag a box. It jumps to the grid lines. Drag it next to another box: a dashed guide line shows and the edges line up.

New: More ways to build the overlay
- **Pictures:** add PNG, JPEG, GIF or BMP pictures up to 200 KB each, and choose how they fill their box.
- **Lock:** a lock stops a piece from being dragged by mistake.
- **Line up:** buttons put a piece at the left, centre or right and the top, middle or bottom of the window. It stays there on any window size.
- **Server part:** the server's part can hold pictures too.
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it ("Add picture", and the boxes of the picked piece)
Test: Add a picture, tick "Can't be dragged", and try to drag it: it stays put. Click "Right" and "Top": it moves to the top-right corner.

New: Overlay add-ons
An add-on is a file (.ahziaeladdon) that holds ready-made widgets (texts, boxes and pictures that go together) and styles (a look to put on any text or box). "Make an add-on from my design" turns your pieces and the picked piece's look into one to share. "Add an add-on file" adds someone else's. Its widgets and styles then show as buttons under Add-ons. An add-on holds only words, colours, numbers and pictures, is checked when it is read, and can't run anything. The Ahziael line can't be changed by one.
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it > Add-ons
Test: Make an add-on from your design, click Start over, then click the add-on's widget button: your pieces come back. Pick a text and click a style: it takes that look.
