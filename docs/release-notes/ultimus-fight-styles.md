Fixed: Ultimus picks how to fight from his own results
Ultimus now sorts each battle by kind, which means how many enemies there are and how strong they are together next to him. He then plays it one of two ways: building up first with a blade or trap and then one big hit, or hitting at once every round. He keeps score of which way really won, in how many rounds and for how much health, and plays the way that does best for that kind of fight, so his battle choices tune themselves instead of needing updates. Until he has 5 fights of a kind each way, he sometimes tries the other way, but only when the enemies are not stronger than him, and heals, shields and finishing blows always come first.
Where: logs\ultimus\fight-styles.txt, each fight line in fights.jsonl, Copy all logs on the Ultimus page in Ahziael, and Rules.FightStyles in his playbook (2 on, 1 watch only, 0 off).
