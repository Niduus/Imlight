# Compact decks and a Trained Spells section

Changed: Ultimus, the Quest Team and Bot Players now build compact decks, the way a good player does. Before, they filled every slot the deck had, so Ultimus carried 34 cards with 6 copies of every shield and charm. Now a deck is about 16 cards (a few more for a boss or a big crowd). The main hits get the most copies, a shield, heal or charm gets only one or two, and a shield goes in only when the enemies can actually hit with its school. Nothing goes in just to fill space, so the best cards come up more often.

New: a Trained Spells section next to the deck in Ahziael, laid out like the game's spellbook. It shows every card the wizard knows, grouped by school. The number on a card is how many copies are in the deck, and faded cards are trained but left out. Other spells (fishing, gardening, travel) are listed by name below.

New: the Bot Players card has a "Bots out now" drop-down that shows each bot's stats, gear, deck and trained spells, like the Quest Team page.

Where: Ahziael > Ultimus page, Quest Team page (pick a wizard), and Game Rules > Battles and World > Bot Players > "Bots out now".

Test:
1. Turn Ultimus on and open his page: his deck should be about 15 to 16 cards, mostly his hits, with one or two of each shield and charm.
2. Look to the right of the deck: Trained spells shows his cards by school, with deck counts on the ones in the deck.
3. Turn Bot Players on, bring one out with .bot test, then open "Bots out now" on the Bot Players card.
