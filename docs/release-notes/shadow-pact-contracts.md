Fixed: Shadow Pacts now show in battle
When a pact spell is cast (Dark Fiend, Dark Shepherd, the Shadow Shrike, Seraph, Sentinel and Trickster shadow selves, and the Shadow fusion spells), its contract now shows on whoever holds it, the same way the real game shows it: hover that wizard or creature in battle and the "Pacts" list shows whose pact it is, what it needs (charm, ward, trap, damage over time and so on) and how many rounds are left. Before, the server kept pacts to itself, so the game never showed a pact at all. A pact cast at one enemy now sits on that enemy and only that enemy feeds it, a new pact from the same caster on the same target replaces the old one, and a pact ends quietly if the enemy holding it is defeated. The chat line when you cast one now also says how much Backlash each missed round costs, and you get a line when the pact ends.
Test: In a battle, cast Shadow Shrike (or any pact spell) and hover your wizard: the Pacts list shows it with 3 rounds, then 2, then 1, then it goes away. Cast Dark Fiend at an enemy and hover that enemy to see the pact there.
