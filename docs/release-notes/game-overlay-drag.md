New: Click and drag on the overlay picture
In "Design it", click a text or box on the picture to pick it, drag it to move it, and drag its right or bottom edge (or the bottom-right corner) to make it wider or taller. The mouse pointer changes to show which one you'll do. The Ahziael line can be dragged to a new place but not sized. The boxes below and the game follow along.
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it (also the "Ahziael Overlay" window)
Test: Drag the Ahziael line up and right, then let go: "In from the side" and "In from the top or bottom" change to match. Drag the right edge of a box: it gets wider and its left side stays put.

New: Building blocks you can drag
Under the list there are two blocks, "Text" and "Box". Drag one onto the picture and let go: a new text or box appears right where you dropped it. Clicking a block adds one at the usual place. You can also drag a line in the list onto another line to change the order (the Ahziael line always stays first).
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it
Test: Drag "Box" to the middle of the picture. Drag the new box's line in the list to the top of your items.

Changed: The kick message says where to get Ahziael
When your server needs the overlay and a player doesn't have it, the standard message now tells them to download Ahziael from github.com/Niduus/Imlight/releases (ImlightManager.exe), add your server under Play, click Play, and log in again. You can still write your own message.
Where: Play > Overlay > Require the overlay on my server
Test: Turn on "Require the overlay", leave the message empty, and log in without the overlay: after the wait, the message shows the download address.
