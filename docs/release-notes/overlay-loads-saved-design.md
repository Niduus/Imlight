Fixed: The overlay page now opens with your saved overlay

After Ahziael opened (for example after an update), the Overlay page showed an empty overlay with nothing ticked, and the first change you made there saved that empty overlay over your real one. Ahziael now reads your saved overlay, its grid settings and your add-ons before it builds any page, so everything you had on is shown and stays on.

Where: Ahziael, Play > Overlay, and the overlay over the game.
Test: Put the Minimap and Quest Tracker on, close Ahziael and open it again (or update). The Overlay page should show both ticked, and they should still be on the game.
