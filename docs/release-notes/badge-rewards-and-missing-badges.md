Fixed: The gift box next to a badge shows its reward
Clicking the gift box beside a badge in your badge book did nothing, because the server never answered. It now shows the badge's reward, such as 500 A-Baahh-Calypse Spellements or a Gold Skeleton Key, and the Claim Reward button gives it to you once. A few rewards (raid house guests, saga boss rematches, "Your School" Chronicle Spellements, guild museum rewards) are not known yet: the badge shows the game's reward text, and claiming tells you the reward is not on the server yet.
Test: Type .givemeallbadges, open your badge book, click the gift box next to a Spellwright badge, then click Claim Reward and check your Spellements.
