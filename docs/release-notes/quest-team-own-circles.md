Fixed: Quest Team kept fleeing battles over their circle order
The Quest Team flee a battle when they are not standing in their order (Ice, Life, Death, Storm), but the server often put them on the wrong circles when they joined. So they fled again and again, and anyone who fled missed the kill and fell behind on the quest step. Each Quest Team wizard now joins on its own circle in the team's order whenever that circle is free.
Where: any Quest Team battle, for example on Unicorn Way during "Ghost Hunters" and "Skeleton Crew".
Test: watch the Quest Team start a few fights and check that they stand Ice, Life, Death, Storm and no longer flee to fix their order.
