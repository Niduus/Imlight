# Quest Tracker overlay add-on

New: Quest Tracker for the game overlay
A new overlay piece lists your wizard's quests in three groups: Story (the main quests you need to finish the world), Main (the other main quests) and Side. Each quest shows its step now, and where it is when it's in another area. Click a quest to pick it: its way is drawn on the Minimap as a line along the streets to the step, with a ring on the spot. Click it again to let it go. Click a group's name to fold it away. The lines show only on the Minimap, so without a Minimap there are no lines. When the step is in another area, the Minimap says where to go and points at the door that leads there. Get it from Niduus's add-ons, or add it in the designer with "Add Quest Tracker".
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Download the Quest Tracker and Minimap add-ons from Niduus's add-ons, click Play, log in, and check your quests show under Story, Main and Side. Click a quest and a line should appear on the Minimap toward its step. Then remove the Minimap and check the line is gone.
