# Quest Tracker scrolls

New: The Quest Tracker list now scrolls when it has more quests than fit. You can turn the mouse wheel over the list, or click the small up and down arrows on its right side. A thin bar between the arrows shows where you are in the list.
New: In the tracker's settings, "Tallest before it scrolls" sets how tall the list gets before it starts to scroll. The default is 320.

Where: Play > Overlay, on the Quest Tracker.

Test:
1. Hold enough quests that the list doesn't fit, or set "Tallest before it scrolls" to something small, like 150.
2. In the game, turn the mouse wheel over the quest list. It should scroll up and down.
3. Click the up and down arrows on the right of the list. Each click should scroll it.
4. Click a quest after scrolling. It should still pick the quest you clicked.
