Fixed: Crystal Storage and other Dragonspyre towers sending you the wrong way
In the Crystal Storage tower ("Pick up the Spare"), the pad that should take you to the next floor sent you back out, and the pad by the entrance took you up instead. The same mix-up was in the Unused Vault in the Grand Chasm, the Secure House in the Hatchery and Firewing's Tower in the Crystal Grove, so all of them were turned the right way round. The Crystal Storage top floor's entrance pad also takes you back out to the Grand Chasm now.
Where: Dragonspyre, Grand Chasm, Hatchery and Crystal Grove towers.
Test: go through the Crystal Storage floor by floor; the pad past each fight goes up a floor and the pad where you came in takes you out.
