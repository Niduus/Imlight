Fixed: Dungeon sigils no longer make the game stutter
Each dungeon sigil in an area played its whole light-up again when you arrived, on your first step, and once more a moment later. An area with several sigils stuttered after every quest that ended with a trip. Each sigil now plays its look once, and the sigil shows the dungeon's real name.
Where: any area with dungeon sigils, such as Moon Cliffs and Triton Avenue.
Test: finish a quest that sends you to an area with several sigils and check that the game does not stutter when you arrive.
