# Quest Tracker: the line goes under arches and roofs

Fixed: The quest line on the minimap went a long way around when an archway, a roof or an overhang was in the way. It treated them like walls even though you can walk under them. Now a wall counts only when it is at the height you walk at. Walls high over your head, or below the ground, no longer block the line.

Where: In the game overlay, use the Quest Tracker together with the Minimap.

Test:
1. Go back to the spot from your screenshot and pick the same quest.
2. Check that the line now goes straight up through the archway instead of around it.
3. Check that the line still goes around real buildings and fences.
