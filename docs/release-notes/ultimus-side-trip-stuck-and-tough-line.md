Fixed: Ultimus no longer gets stuck on a shopping trip or says he needs healing at full health
Ultimus stood for a long time at the door of Jack Russell Jewelers in Regent's Square trying to buy a ring, and wrote it up as a broken step of his story quest. Now, if he gets stuck on any side trip (a shop, a trainer, selling or the Bazaar), he drops it at once, leaves that place alone for a few hours and goes back to his story. Shop doors whose entrance is behind the door are now clicked at the door, as a player does. He also said "This one's tough, healing up first" at full health on his way to a shop: the extra care before tough fights now only applies to fights, and he only says it when he really goes to heal, naming mana when that is what is low.
Where: Ultimus, on by default; playbook rule Guard.TripOutOfReachHours (3).
Test: Watch Ultimus go shopping in Marleybone. He should get into Jack Russell Jewelers, or, if he gets stuck, drop the trip with a pink SIDE TRIP DROPPED line and go back to his quest. At full health he should never say he is healing up.
