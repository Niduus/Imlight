New: Quest way on a small map, remembered quest pick, and world names for the Quest Team

The Ultimus and Quest Team pages now show a small map next to the quest box, with the way to the quest they play, just like the spectator map on the game. Click any quest in the page's Quest Tracker to see that quest's way instead, and click it again to go back. The quest you pick in your own Quest Tracker over the game is now remembered, so it is still picked after Ahziael restarts or updates. The Quest Team's quest box on its page now shows the quest's world by its full name, like Ultimus's does.

Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page and Quest Team page, and the Quest Tracker over the game.
Test: With Ultimus playing, open his page and click a different quest in the Quest Tracker there; the map should draw its way. Pick a quest in your own Quest Tracker, close and open Ahziael, and it should still be picked.
