# Ultimus heals first, answers whispers, turns down hatching, picks solo Balance gear

Fixed: Ultimus walked back into fights with little or no health. He used to rest for a minute and then fight anyway. Now healing comes before anything else, the way a player does it:
1. He drinks a potion if he has one.
2. Otherwise he walks to a health (or mana) wisp in the area that no creature stands near.
3. Otherwise he goes to a potion shop and fills his potions with his gold. He uses the one in the area if there is one, or travels to the nearest area of the world that has one.
4. If none of those is possible, he keeps talking to NPCs and travelling (walking around creatures) but does not start a fight. After 10 minutes with no way at all to heal, the log says so in pink and he fights on, so he never freezes forever.

New: Ultimus answers whispers. Whisper to him by Menu Chat or typed chat and he whispers back a fitting Menu Chat phrase. He says hello back, "You're welcome" to thanks and "Goodbye" to bye. He says no to asks that change his plan, says he's questing when asked what he's doing, and says he's busy to anything else.

New: Ultimus turns down every pet hatching request. The Quest Team does too.

Changed: Ultimus picks his gear for questing alone. That means more weight on health, resist, block and incoming healing, since nobody else heals him. Damage and pierce now count for the schools his hits actually land as. Balance's Hydra, Spectral Blast and Nested Fury hit as Fire, Ice or Storm, and Chimera as Life, Death or Myth. He works this out from his own deck, so gear for those schools counts by how much of his damage they carry.

Where: no settings. The Ultimus log shows "is hurt ... heals before going on", "picking up a health wisp", "fills its potions at the potion shop", "whispers back in Menu Chat" and "turns down a pet hatching request".

Test:
1. Let Ultimus lose most of his health in a fight. He should drink a potion, pick up a wisp or visit a potion shop before he walks to the next creature.
2. Whisper "Hello" to Ultimus in Menu Chat. He should whisper back.
3. Ask Ultimus to hatch a pet. It should be declined at once.
4. Check his gear on the Ultimus page after he levels. It should lean toward Balance gear with good health and resist.
