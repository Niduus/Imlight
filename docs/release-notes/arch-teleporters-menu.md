Fixed: Arch teleporters opened a "What can I do for you?" menu instead of taking you through
Some arch teleporters, like the ones in the Tower Archives, offered two choices at once, so pressing X showed a "What can I do for you?" menu like a shop keeper's. Now the teleporter offers only its one real choice and takes you straight through, for every teleporter of this kind in every world.
Where: Arch teleporters such as the Tower Archives arches in Dragonspyre.
Test: Walk up to an arch in the Tower Archives and press X. You go straight through with no menu.
