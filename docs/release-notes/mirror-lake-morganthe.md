Fixed: Mirror Lake left you standing in the wrong lake so Morganthe could not be fought
On Chasing Shadows the sigil in Baobab Market takes you to Mirror Lake, and the lake is meant to send you straight on to the drained Mirror Lake where Morganthe waits. That last hop had no place to land, so you stayed in the normal lake with nobody to fight. Now it takes you to the drained Mirror Lake in your own copy.
Where: Mirror Lake in Zafaria, quest Chasing Shadows.
Test: on Chasing Shadows, step on the Mirror Lake sigil in Baobab Market and check that you land in the drained lake and can fight Morganthe's step.
