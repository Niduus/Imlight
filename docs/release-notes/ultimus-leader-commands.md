New: Ultimus follows commands from his group leader

When Ultimus is in your group and you are the leader, you can tell him what to do in chat, and anyone else is politely told that he only listens to the leader. Out of battle he understands stay here, follow me, come to me, leave the group and do your own thing. In battle he understands let me go first, you go first, pass, flee, draw a treasure card, discard a card, heal, shield, blade, trap or attack with a name (like heal me, shield Mitsu or attack the boss), and cast a card. Saying cast Dragonblade makes him think about it, while cast Dragonblade on me or on MoMi makes him cast it on that wizard if the card is in his hand, and you can join several with and or then, like let me go first then heal me.

Where: In game chat, with Ultimus in your group.
Test: Group with Ultimus as the leader and type stay here, then follow me. In a battle type let me go first then cast a card from his hand on you, and see him wait for your pick and then cast it on you.
