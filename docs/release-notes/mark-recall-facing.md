Fixed: Recall faces the way you set your Mark
When you placed a Mark and then went to another area, or logged out and back in, recalling to it turned you to a random direction instead of the way you were facing when you placed it. The server was sending the Mark's height in place of its facing for those recalls. Now the facing is kept with the Mark at all times, so a recall always lands you facing the same way, from any area and after a relog.
Where: In game, Mark Location and Recall Location, no setting.
Test: Face a wall and place a Mark. Go to a different area, then Recall: you land facing that wall. Log out and back in somewhere else, Recall again: same facing.
