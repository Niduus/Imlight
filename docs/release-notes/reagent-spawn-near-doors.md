Fixed: Teleporters and doors gave Mist Wood instead of taking you through
Reagents could appear right on top of a teleporter or door, like the Firecat Alley entrance, so pressing X picked up Mist Wood instead of going through, and bots got stuck pressing it. Reagents now appear away from doors, teleporters, dungeon pads and people you can talk to, in every zone of every world.
Where: Every zone with reagents.
Test: Go to the Firecat Alley entrance in Wizard City and press X on the teleporter. It takes you through every time.
