# Ultimus thinks smarter on the way

New: He picks up wisps after a fight. When a fight he won leaves health or mana wisps nearby and he is not full, he walks over and picks them up before going on.
New: He heals right before a fight. With a fight next, if he is under 90% health or 60% mana and a wisp is close by, he takes it first.
New: He does what's in front of him. If another quest he holds has a step right by him (a person, a creature, a thing to use) while his main step is a long walk away or in another area, he does that one first. That includes before he walks off to turn a quest in.
New: The best creature for kill steps. Alone, he picks a creature standing apart, so others don't join the battle. When he is well over the quest's level, healthy, and needs more than one, he takes a few wanted ones together.
New: He seeks or avoids fights depending on the step. When he is short of the level his story needs and healthy, creatures on his way are XP: he no longer walks round them. Otherwise he still avoids fights he doesn't need.
New: Faster teleporter paths. When a street teleporter gets him to a step much faster than walking (under 60% of the walk, on long walks), he takes it, and the Quest Team does too.
Changed: Learned shortcuts are every bot's default. When more than one proven walk is known, all bots now take the shortest one that works. Teleporters a bot learned count for every bot.
(The closest-target-first order for steps with several targets came in the previous part of this patch.)

Where: Nothing to click. You see it in his log and Thoughts: "picks up a health wisp after the fight", "for another quest of mine is right here", "finds a faster way ... than walking".

Test: Let Ultimus fight in Wizard City. After a win with a wisp nearby, he should walk over to it. When under-leveled, he should fight what's on his way instead of walking round it.
