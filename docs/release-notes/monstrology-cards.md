New: Give yourself Monstrology cards
A new Monstrology tab lists every Summon Monster and Expel Monster treasure card the real game has, by monster. Pick one, choose how many and press Give to my wizard. The same works in the game's chat: .monstercard summon Lost Soul 3 or .monstercard expel Lost Soul 1. Only real Monstrology cards are given, and your treasure card book still holds at most 999 cards.
Where: Progress > Monstrology
Test: Log in with your wizard, open the tab, search Lost Soul, pick Summon Lost Soul, set 2 and press Give to my wizard. Open your spellbook's treasure cards: there are 2 more. Then type .monstercard expel Lost Soul 1 in the chat.
