New: Compare the Quest Team's AIs, let them chat among themselves, and give them their own per-player limit
A new "Ask all four" button asks Freya, Anubis, Terra and Chaac the same question, each with their own AI, and shows their answers side by side with the service, model and how long each took. With Quest Team AI chat on, two of them standing together out of battle now and then say a short line to each other in their own way, at most once every few minutes (10 by default, 0 turns it off). The team also has its own limit of AI answers per player per day, counted apart from Ultimus's, so talking to the team doesn't use up a player's answers from Ultimus.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus AI Chat page, Quest Team AI chat.
Test: With the server running, type a question and click "Ask all four", then read the four answers below it. Turn Quest Team AI chat on, stand near the team for about ten minutes and watch two of them talk, then set the per-player box to 1 and see the team give you its usual answer after one AI reply.
