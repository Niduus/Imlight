New: Ultimus tells players what he is up to
Ultimus now says a short line in chat at moments that matter: before leaving for a while (a trip for potions or the Bazaar), when he is stuck and asks for the way, at big moments like a level up or a finished world, when he joins or leaves a group, and before a tough fight. He only speaks when a player is near him, or whispers his group mate, and he keeps it rare so it never spams. Private details are always blocked, as before.
Where: Ultimus, in game chat, no setting.
Test: Stand near Ultimus while he plays. You should see a line now and then, for example "Level 16! Woo!" or "I'm stuck on ... Anyone know how to do it?".
