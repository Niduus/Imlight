New: Every page in a side bar on the left
All pages and their tabs are listed on the left, so nothing hides behind a menu. Star a tab with Pin to keep it at the top, your last tabs are under Recent, and Back, Forward and the "Quests › My Quests" line over each tab show where you are.
Test: Click a few tabs in the side bar, then use Back and Forward (or Alt+Left and Alt+Right). Press Pin on a tab and check it shows under Pinned, also after closing and opening Ahziael.

New: Search everything from the top of any page
Click the search box at the top, or press Ctrl+K, and a search window opens over the page: pages, settings, commands, creatures, NPCs, quests, spells, items, places and patch notes, with how many of each. Pick one to see it on the right, where you can change a setting, teleport to a place or copy a command without leaving.
Test: Press Ctrl+K and type "aggro", pick the setting and change it, then Save changes. Type "triton", pick Triton Avenue and press Teleport my wizard here while playing.

New: Real patch notes
What's New now sorts every change into New, Fixed and Changed in plain words, with a Show me button that opens where it is, How to test steps and a Tested tick so you know what is left to try. "Since your last update" gathers everything new since the version you had before.
Where: Help > What's New
Test: Open Help > What's New, click New, Fixed and Changed, and tick Tested on a note. Close and open Ahziael and check the tick stayed.
