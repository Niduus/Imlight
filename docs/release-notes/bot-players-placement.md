# Bot Players: natural places and fitting levels

Fixed: Street bots no longer appear in ponds or in random spots off the paths. They come in on the paths, away from water, mostly where you can see them and in the open places where players gather (squares, wide streets, shop fronts). When they walk around they stay on paths and grass and never cross water.

Fixed: Street bots have levels that fit the world. A world's bots are between the level players reach it and its level cap, and a few are slightly over the cap (Azteca: about 80 to 93, never 177). When you are at that world's levels, its bots are close to your own level.

Where: Any street with you in it, while Bot Players and "Fill the streets" are on.

Test:
1. Go to a street with a pond or lake (for example the Commons or Unicorn Way) and wait for bots to come in. None should stand in the water, and they should keep to the paths.
2. With a high level wizard, go to Azteca. The bots there should be around levels 80 to 93.
3. Go to Wizard City. Its bots should be low levels (about 1 to 18).
