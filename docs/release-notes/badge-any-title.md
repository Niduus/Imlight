Changed: Wear any earned badge as your title
Most badges (Spellwright, boss and quest badges) are marked by the game as "no title", so equipping them was refused. Now any badge you have earned can be worn with the Equip button in its badge window. Turn AnyBadgeAsTitle off under [Badges] to go back to the game's rule; it applies right away.
Test: Open your badge book, click a finished Spellwright badge, click Equip, and check the title over your wizard.
