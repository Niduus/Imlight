New: Ahziael overlay over the game
While Ahziael is open, "Ahziael made by Niduus | version" shows in the bottom-left corner of the Wizard101 window and follows it when you move or resize it. Clicks go straight through to the game, and nothing in the game or its files is changed. It shows while the game window is in front, in windowed mode; true full screen hides every other window, so it can't show there.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Click Play Wizard101, put the game in a window, and check the line in the bottom-left. Move and resize the game window and check the line follows it.

New: Design your own overlay, no code
Add your own texts and boxes over the game and change everything about them: font, size, bold, italic, underline, letter spacing, one colour or a straight or round gradient with up to three colours, background, edge, round corners, shadow or glow, see-through, turn, and which corner or side it is pinned to. Words can show the version, server, time or date. The picture on the tab shows the change at once, and designs can be saved to a file and shared (they hold only colours, words and numbers, so they can't run anything). The Ahziael line can be restyled but always stays in its corner.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Click Add text, type a word, give it a gradient and a shadow, then look at the game. Save the design to a file, click Start over, then open the file again.

New: Server switch to require the overlay
A switch (off to start) makes your server disconnect any player whose PC has no Ahziael overlay running, with a message you can change. Ahziael on the player's PC tells the server every 5 seconds that it is drawing on the game, over the Remote Admin port (12410), with no account or password. It works at once, no restart, also for players already in the game. Friends on the internet need that port open, so click "Let friends connect" again after turning it on.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Turn on "Require the Ahziael overlay" and play (you stay in). Then, while in the game, turn off "Show the overlay" on the same tab: within about half a minute you are disconnected with the message. Turn it back on and log in again.
