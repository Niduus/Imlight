Fixed: Cheat blocks can be grabbed anywhere to drag them
Blocks inside a cheat only moved when you grabbed the tiny dotted grip on their left, so click and drag on the block itself did nothing. Now you can grab a block anywhere (except its boxes and buttons) and drag it up or down to reorder it. The page also scrolls by itself when you drag near its top or bottom edge, and dragging a block from the palette into the cheat works the same way.
Where: Create > Player Cheats
Test: Open a cheat with Edit blocks, grab one of its Do blocks by its words and drag it above another Do block, then let go. Also drag a block from the list on the left into the middle of the cheat.
