New: Turn questing street bots on and off in Ahziael
The Street Bots page has a new Questing street bots card. It turns on street bots that play the Main Story like the TCO7, sets how many quest at once (up to 30), and shows each one's name, school, level, area and quest. You can also let them help each other in street fights and choose how often they try a different way past a spot they are stuck on. Every change works right away, without a restart.
Where: Bots > Street Bots
Test: Turn Questing street bots on with 3, start the server, and after a minute three bots should show on the card with their level and quest.

New: The Hive mind lists what failed, not only what worked
Each quest in the Hive mind now also shows what to avoid: moves that did not get a bot unstuck at a step, moves that only worked some of the time, and problems the bots ran into there, like losing the same fight again and again. The questing street bots' logs are read too.
Where: Hive mind
Test: Open the Hive mind tab after the bots have played a while, and look for Avoid, Try last and Went wrong lines under a quest.
