Fixed: Teleports never leave you stuck inside a pillar, wall or furniture
Every teleport to a spot now checks the floor there first: Quest Team TP, TP buttons, .tp to a wizard, street teleporters and going back after a battle. If the spot is inside a pillar, wall or piece of furniture, or has no floor, you land on the nearest open floor beside it instead. With no open floor nearby you go to the area's start. Pillars and posts were also measured wrong before (flat and wide instead of tall and thin), so bots and street teleporters now steer round them properly too. You can turn the check off with SafeLanding in the [World] part of Imlight.ini; the change applies at once.
Test: Start the Quest Team, then press TP on its page a few times while its wizards are indoors (like the Headmistress's house). You should always land on open floor and be able to walk.
