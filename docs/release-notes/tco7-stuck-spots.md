Fixed: Bots stop going round in circles at Triton Avenue's teleporters and potion trips

A battle that caught a bot on its way to a teleporter used to make it give up on that teleporter, so it wandered the long way round for half an hour (Suzie Gryphonbane and the Tempest Nexus in Triton Avenue). Time spent in a battle no longer counts, and a bot standing on a teleporter that has not fired steps off and walks in again, as a player would. A bot filling potions before a dungeon no longer Recalls back the moment it lands in The Commons before it has bought them. Quest times picked up halfway after a restart are no longer saved as the quest's time, so the TCO7 are compared with Ultimus fairly.

Where: Ultimus, the TCO7 and the Quest Team, everywhere.
Test: Watch a TCO7 member do The Enemy Revealed and To Tame a Tempest in Triton Avenue and check it reaches Suzie Gryphonbane and the Tempest Nexus without going back to The Commons over and over.
