Changed: Ultimus and the Quest Team spend training points on what they lack most in fights
Each wizard now weighs what its own fights show it lacks most. When it keeps losing or ends fights nearly beaten it saves for Satyr, when single hits take a big share of its health it saves for Tower Shield, and when fights drag on it saves for Feint. Its plan card says what it weighed and why, and the earlier rules (school shields, no doubling up with teammates, pins) still apply.
