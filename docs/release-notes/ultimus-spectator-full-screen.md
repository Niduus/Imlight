New: Full screen and zoom on Ultimus's minimap
The Ultimus Spectator's own minimap now has the same buttons as your Minimap: full screen, + (closer) and - (farther). Full screen shows Ultimus's map over the game with his quest line, and X takes it back to the corner. Only one map is full screen at a time, so opening Ultimus's closes yours and the other way round. The zoom you pick is kept on this PC.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: With Ultimus Spectator on, click the full screen button on the corner of the purple Ultimus map, use + and -, then X. Also try + and - on the small Ultimus map.
