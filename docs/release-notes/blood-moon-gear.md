Added: Blood Moon gear
Gear made in Blood Moon's Make window now works on the server. Each piece is the game's QA item for its slot, looks like that QA item unless another look was picked, and while worn it counts the stats set in Blood Moon instead of the QA item's own, plus any cards put on it. The game's hover box still shows the QA item's name and numbers; the stats page shows the real ones. Type .customgear to list the pieces and .customgear and a number to get one.
Where: Blood Moon, Make, Gear; in the game, .customgear.
Test: make a hat in Blood Moon with +50% Fire damage, press Give it to my wizard, wear it, and check the stats page shows 50% more Fire damage.
