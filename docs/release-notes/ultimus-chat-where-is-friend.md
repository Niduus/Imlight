New: Ultimus can tell you where his friends are
Asking Ultimus "where is" a wizard on his friends list now gets the area they are in, the way a player sees it on the friends list. For wizards who are not his friends he says he can't see where they are, and quest people are still answered from the game's own area files, even in places he hasn't been.
Where: Ultimus, when you ask him "where is" someone in the game.
Test: Make friends with Ultimus, then ask him "where is" and your wizard's name, and check that he names your area.
