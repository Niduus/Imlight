Added: Copy, move or link a player cheat to a wizard or account
Each player cheat in the list has a new link button. Copy to makes a second cheat just for the wizard or account you pick, to change on its own. Move to gives the cheat to them instead of whoever had it. Link to adds them, so one cheat is shared and a change to it counts for everyone linked. The Who gets this cheat box also has a new choice for a mix of wizards, accounts and you.
Where: Player Cheats page in Ahziael, the link button beside each cheat.
Test: Press the link button on a cheat, pick Link to and a friend's account, press Do it, then Save player cheats. The cheat's line says it is for you and that account.
