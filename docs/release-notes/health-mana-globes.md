# Health, Mana and Energy globes on the wizard pages

Fixed: the Health and Mana icons on the Ultimus, Quest Team and Bot Players pages were the wrong pictures. Now they are the globes Niduus picked from the game's own HUD art: the red liquid ball in the glass globe for Health, the blue one for Mana, and the green one in the clawed globe for Energy. Ahziael puts them together from the game files on your PC, so nothing from the game is shipped.

Changed: the glass globe now sits in front of the liquid, the liquid fills the sides of the ball, and it is trimmed at the top. The liquid is solid and bright as in the game, with only the glass shine and outline over it  Health is full red, as the game shows it (Niduus, 2026-11-13).

Where: Ahziael > Ultimus page and Quest Team page (Stats), and the Bot Players card's "Bots out now".
