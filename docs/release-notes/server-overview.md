New: Overview of who's online
A new tab shows everyone on your server right now: the wizard they are on, its level and school, their account, where they are, whether they are in a battle or exploring, and how long they have been on. Under it is a list of the accounts that were online last, with when, on which wizard and where.
Where: Server > Overview
Test: Start the server, log in with a wizard, then open Server > Overview. Walk to another area or start a battle and watch the tab change by itself.

Fixed: Accounts said "Last online: Never"
The server now saves when each account logs in and when it was last online, on which wizard and where. The Accounts tab shows it, or "Online now" for someone who is on. Accounts that have not logged in since this update still say Never until their next login.
Where: Server > Accounts
Test: Log in, play a minute, log out, then open Server > Accounts and click Refresh.
