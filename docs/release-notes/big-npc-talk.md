Fixed: You can talk to NPCs that are much bigger than normal
The game shows no talk prompt for an NPC that stands far bigger than normal, such as the Auroracle over its pool, so the talk step could not be done. Walking up to such an NPC on a talk step for it now opens the talk by itself, once per step.
Where: Polaris, the Auroracle's chamber, and any other oversized NPC.
Test: on the step to talk to the Auroracle, walk up to the pool and check that the talk opens.
