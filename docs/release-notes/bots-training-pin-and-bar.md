New: Training plan points bar and Pin button in Ahziael
Each Ultimus and Quest Team wizard card now shows a bar of how many training points it has saved against how many its plan needs. You can also pick a spell in the list and press Pin to choose its training goal by hand, and press Unpin to let it plan from its own fights again.
Where: Ahziael, the Ultimus page and the Quest Team page, under each wizard's name.
Test: Open the Quest Team page, pick Tower Shield for one wizard and press Pin; within a few seconds its plan should say you pinned Tower Shield.
