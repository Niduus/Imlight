Fixed: TCO7 members speak in their own names

The TCO7 members' problem notes and reports used to say "Ultimus" even when they were about Tulkar, Cyndr or another member. Each member's notes, progress report and session report now carry that member's own name, and the rivals list on a member's report says it is the creature book the whole team shares. A member that is waiting for its group mate to finish a battle is no longer flagged as stuck while it waits.

Where: Ahziael, TCO7 section, any member's Issues and Logs.
Test: Turn the TCO7 on, let them play a while, then open a member's tab and check the issues and reports name that member.
