# Ultimus guardrails: he stays on his goal

New:
- **Mission check.** His mission is "Finish the main story solo, like a human." Every new move is checked against it, and his Thoughts say why it serves the story ("Next: ..., because ..."). A move he can't give a story reason for is turned down and written down.
- **Off-track alarm.** If he picks up a quest that isn't the story's, or makes no story progress for 15 minutes, he stops, says why, drops any side trip and plans again toward the story. Story progress means a step, a quest, a level, a won fight for a step or for XP, or reaching the area he meant to.
- **Hard limits only you can change.** No step skipping; no free XP, gold or items; no crafting or pet training; staying in bounds; side areas only when a quest names them. His daily review can never change these, or his budgets.
- **Time budgets.**
  - A step gets 20 minutes. After that he sets it aside, does another open step first, or finds another way.
  - A quest gets 4 times its expected time, at least 30 minutes, not counting levelling up for it. After that he plans it again.
  - A side trip (shop, Bazaar, trainer, selling) gets 8 minutes. After that he drops it, and side trips close for 30 minutes.
- **Risk budget.** A fight he trusts under 70 out of 100 isn't started until he is ready: full health and mana, and a deck made for it.
- **Progress check every 5 minutes.** He asks "am I closer to my goal than 5 minutes ago?". After two noes in a row he switches strategy.
- **Ahziael controls.**
  - Ultimus tab: a new **Back to story** button next to Pause. He drops any side trip, a pause is lifted, and he heads back to his story quest.
  - New **His own numbers** card: turn self-tuning on or off, and lock any number he may tune (health before a step, when he heals in battle, when he plays it careful). Locked numbers are left alone at his daily review.
- "Pause and explain" now shows his mission, his hard limits, whether side trips are open, and how long since his last story progress.
- All the new numbers are in his playbook (Guard section, logs\ultimus\playbook.txt). You can change them there, and he never changes them himself.

Where: Ahziael > Ultimus tab. His Thoughts and log.
