New: Parents and children in the overlay
Any text, box, picture or button can belong to another piece. Pick the other piece under "Belongs to (moves with it)". The child is then pinned to a corner or side of its parent instead of the game window. It moves when the parent moves, and it's hidden while the parent is hidden. A child can have children of its own. In the list, children show indented under their parent with ↳. Copying a piece copies its children too. Removing a piece removes its children (Ahziael asks first). Add-on widgets keep their families. The Ahziael line can't be a parent or a child.
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it (the picked piece's "Belongs to"; on the game, the pick list in the bar)
Test: Add a box and a text. Set the text's "Belongs to" to the box and drag the box: the text goes with it. Untick the box's "Shown": the text hides too.

Changed: Grid view and snapping each on their own
The grid view, snapping to the grid and snapping to other pieces each turn on or off separately. On the game, the bar has "Grid", "Snap to grid", "Snap to pieces" and "Grid settings" (size, colours, see-through, stronger lines, snap distance, guide lines).
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it > Grid and snapping, and the bar on the game
Test: On the game, untick "Snap to grid" with the grid still showing: pieces drag freely. Open "Grid settings" and change the square size: the grid changes at once.
