New: Spectate Anyone overlay add-on
A new overlay add-on lets the host watch anyone in the game, players and bots alike: Ultimus, the TCO7 and the Quest Team. It shows their minimap with their quest's way, their quest box with everyone's names to click, and a bot's spells in battle. Next and Back go through everyone, and Go to takes you next to them. It only works for the host, on the PC that runs the server.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Download Spectate Anyone (and the Minimap add-on), start the game on the server's PC and click Next a few times. The map and quest box switch to each wizard in turn.
