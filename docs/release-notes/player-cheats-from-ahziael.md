New: Use your cheats from Ahziael on any server
Playing on someone else's Imlight server? Sign in once with your game username and password, and Ahziael shows your player cheats there. Turn them on or off, use the ones made as buttons, and run any chat command you're allowed, all without typing in chat. Your password is never saved: the server gives your PC a secret key, every command is signed with it and numbered so nobody can fake or repeat one, and it only works while your wizard is in the game from that same PC.
Where: Play > Overlay > My cheats on this server
Test: Start the game on a server with Play, log in a wizard, then sign in on this card and click "Show my cheats". Type .cheats in the "Run a command" box and press Enter: the same answer shows in the game.

New: Cheats you press like a button
Cheats have a new When block, "it is pressed (a button)". A player uses it with the Use button in Ahziael, a button on their overlay, or by typing .cheat use and the cheat's name; in a battle it goes off at the start of the round or right after the next move. It works on creatures too: give the creature's cheat a Name, and it goes off when a wizard in its battle presses a cheat of that name.
Where: Create > Player Cheats
Test: Make a player cheat with "When it is pressed", name it Shield Me, and give it a Do that casts Tower Shield on you. In a battle type .cheat use Shield Me (or click Use in Ahziael): the shield goes on at the start of the round.

New: Host switch for cheats from Ahziael
Hosts can turn off players' cheats and commands from Ahziael with "Let players use their cheats from Ahziael". It's on to start, and players can only do what they could type in chat anyway. Applies right away.
Where: Play > Overlay > Require the overlay on my server
Test: Turn it off, then click "Show my cheats" on a player's PC: it says the host turned it off.
