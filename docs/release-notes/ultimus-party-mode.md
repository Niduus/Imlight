New: Ultimus can join your group
When you invite Ultimus to a group, he now joins and goes along with you. If you are in another area he teleports to you from the group, like any player. He walks into your battles, does the quest steps you both have, and otherwise stays close. When the group ends, or you are away for 15 minutes, he says goodbye and goes back to his own story. Asking him to group in chat now gets a yes.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page, Party mode switch (on by default).
Test: Invite Ultimus to a group in the game. He should join, come to you, and walk into your next battle.
