New: Ultimus learns from his AI answers
When the AI answers a player's Wizard101 question, like where to get Tower Shield or which school suits a world, Ultimus now keeps that answer and uses it in his normal chat later, so he can still answer with the AI off, paused, used up or without internet. He keeps only game knowledge: never his quest or place of that moment, never the player's name, and only answers that passed the safety check, which checks them again each time he says one. The AI may now also answer game questions from its own Wizard101 knowledge instead of saying it is not sure, and he keeps how a group leader worded a command once the AI worked out which command it was. A new "What Ultimus learned from the AI" part of the AI Chat page lists everything he learned, with a Remove button on each and switches to turn learning off.
Where: Ultimus's chat in game, and Ahziael's Ultimus AI Chat page.
