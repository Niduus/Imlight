New: Bazaar bot on/off switch
The Bazaar bot can now be turned off. While it is off the Bazaar is closed, and talking to Elik says "The Bazaar is disabled." (you can change those words on the Announcements tab, group Bazaar). While it is on, the bot only runs when a player is in the Bazaar, and goes to sleep a few minutes after the last player leaves. No restart needed.
Where: Game Rules > Shops and Codes
Test: Turn "Bazaar bot" off, talk to Elik in Olde Town: you get the "disabled" box and no Bazaar window. Turn it on, walk into the Bazaar, wait about a minute, then talk to Elik: the Bazaar opens. Leave for 5 minutes and the card says "Asleep".

New: Bazaar mode, Unlimited or Actual
Unlimited is the Bazaar as it was: everything is always in stock. Actual works like the real game: the Bazaar starts empty, only what players sell to it is for sale, and each buy takes it away. No Auction, No Trade and Crowns-only items are never put up. Switching between the two keeps both stocks, and it applies right away.
Where: Game Rules > Shops and Codes
Test: Pick "Actual", open the Bazaar: it is empty. Sell a hat to Elik, then look in the Hats tab: it is there with 1 in stock. Buy it back: it is gone again. Pick "Unlimited": everything is back.

Changed: Ultimus and the Bazaar
Ultimus checks whether the Bazaar is on and what it really has. He skips it while it is turned off, waits about a minute while it wakes up, and in Actual mode only goes for gear someone actually sold. If another player buys it first, he drops that trip instead of trying again.
Where: Ultimus > Logs
Test: Turn the Bazaar to Actual with nothing sold, and watch Ultimus reach a new world: he does not walk to the Bazaar.
