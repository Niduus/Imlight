Fixed: Teleport stones a quest asks you to use

Some quest steps ask you to use a thing more times than there are of it in the area, such as using Survey Camp's teleport stone 3 times in The Big Guy in Celestia. The first use took the stone away for you, so the step stopped at 1 of 3 and could not be finished. Now a teleporter always stays after a quest use, and so does a thing that is the only one of its kind there when the step needs more uses, so every use counts.

Where: Celestia, Survey Camp, The Big Guy, and any quest step that asks for repeated uses of a teleporter or of a single thing in an area.

Test: On The Big Guy, after the Optio fights, use the Survey Camp teleport stone, come back and use it again until the step shows 3 of 3.
