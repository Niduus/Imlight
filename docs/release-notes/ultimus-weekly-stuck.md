New: Ultimus weekly stuck report
The top of Ultimus's Daily diary now lists the 5 places he lost the most time over the last 7 days, how many days each one held him back, and the fix he would suggest. Once a week, the first time he plays in a new week, last week's report is also written to his log and saved as weekly-stuck.txt in his Ultimus folder. It builds up from the day it is installed, so the first full report comes after a week of play.
