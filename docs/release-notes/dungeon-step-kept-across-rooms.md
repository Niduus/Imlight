Fixed: A dungeon quest step done in one room is no longer undone in another
In Katzenstein's Lab, Ultimus beat Clanker, but walking back into the lab's first room undid the win. The game then sent him back to fight a Clanker that was already gone, and he went back and forth between two rooms. This happened because the first room had closed while nobody was in it, so the dungeon never heard about the win, and on the way back in his quest was swapped for the dungeon's older copy. Now a closed room still counts as part of the dungeon, and anyone who is ahead of the dungeon keeps their progress and moves the rest of the group on with them. A quest saved from an earlier run of a dungeon still starts fresh, as before.
Where: Every dungeon with more than one room, for players, Ultimus and the Quest Team.
Test: Let Ultimus play Katzenstein's Lab again; after he beats Clanker he should go back to Watson and finish "Gate is Enough". The server log should never say "Replaced saved dungeon quest" for a quest he was ahead on.
