Fixed: Top of the Rock can be reached through Nemean Rock Cave

In Top of the Rock you open the Tsavo Door and go through Nemean Rock Cave to reach the top of the rock, where King Mansa is caged. The way out at the far end of the cave sent wizards back outside to the Savannah instead of up to the top, so nobody could play the drum by the King's Cage and the quest could not be finished. That far end now takes you to the top of Nemean Rock.
Where: Zafaria, Savannah, the quest Top of the Rock (step "Play Drum" near King's Cage).
Test: Take Top of the Rock to the Play Drum step, go through the Tsavo Door into Nemean Rock Cave, walk to its far end, and check that you arrive at the top of the rock next to King Mansa's cage.
