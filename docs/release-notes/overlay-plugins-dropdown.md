New: Plugins drop-down and a shorter Overlay page

The Overlay page has a Plugins list at the top with every piece from your add-ons, such as the Minimap, Quest Tracker and the Ultimus and Quest Team Spectators, each marked "on" when it is on your overlay. Pick one and click "Put on the overlay" or "Take it off". Niduus's add-ons you have not downloaded yet are in the list too, and putting one on downloads it first. The long help words moved behind the question marks, and the grid, add-ons and server sections now start folded.

Where: Ahziael, Play > Overlay.
Test: Open the Plugins list, pick "Quest Tracker", and click "Put on the overlay". It should show on the picture and on the game, and read "(on)" in the list.
