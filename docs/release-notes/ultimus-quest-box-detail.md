Changed: Ultimus's quest box shows more detail
The Ultimus Quest box in the Ultimus Spectator add-on now says what kind of quest he is on (story, main, dungeon or side), the quest's level and its world. It lists every open step with its count, like "Defeat Ice Weaver (2 of 4)", instead of only the first three. It also names the other quests he holds instead of only counting them.
Where: Play > Overlay, the Ultimus Quest box from the Ultimus Spectator add-on.
Test: Watch Ultimus with the Ultimus Spectator add-on on. Under his quest's name you should see a line like "Story quest · level 17 · Krokotopia", then all of its steps, and at the bottom "Also holds:" with quest names.
