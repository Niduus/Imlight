New: Ultimus and Quest Team quests shown just like in the game

The Ultimus and Quest Team pages now draw the quest box and the Quest Tracker exactly as they look over the game, in your overlay's own colours. You see the quest they play now with each step, its count and where, the story count and time to the next world, and every quest they hold under Story, Main and Side. The overlay designer also shows your real quests and the real Ultimus or Quest Team box while you play, instead of made-up ones. The old text version is still there, folded, for copying.

Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page and Quest Team page (under the wizard's name), and Play > Overlay.
Test: With Ultimus or the Quest Team playing, open its page. The quest box and Quest Tracker should match what the spectator shows on the game.
