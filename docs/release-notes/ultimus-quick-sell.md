New: Ultimus quick sells when his backpack is full and no shop is near

When his backpack is almost full and there is no shop close by, Ultimus now uses Quick Sell from his backpack, the same as a player does. He only sells the things he would never wear, and he never does it in the middle of a battle.

Where: Ultimus, while questing.
Test: Fill Ultimus's backpack with gear he can't use while he is far from a shop, and watch his Thoughts and gold after he quick sells.
