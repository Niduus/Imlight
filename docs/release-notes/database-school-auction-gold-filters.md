New: Search and sort by Moon, Sun, Star and Shadow
The School filter on the Gear, Items and Spells tabs now always lists Moon, Sun, Star and Shadow after the seven main schools. Gear and Items can now be sorted by school too, and the Spells tab's school sort now goes in game order (Fire to Balance, then Moon, Sun, Star, Shadow).
Where: Database > Gear
Test: On the Spells tab pick School: Star. On the Gear tab pick "School of its item cards: Shadow", then Sort by: School.

New: Find items that can or can't be auctioned
A new "Bazaar (Auction House)" filter on the Gear, Items and Furniture tabs shows only items that can be sold at the Bazaar, or only items that can't (the game's No Auction mark).
Where: Database > Items
Test: Open Filters, pick "Can't be auctioned", and check that the items listed show the No Auction mark.

New: Find items that cost only gold
A new "Costs gold only" tick on the Gear, Items and Furniture tabs shows only items with a gold price and nothing else: no Crowns, no Arena tickets and no PvP coins. Tick "Sold by a vendor" too to see only what shops sell for gold. The Gear tab now has "Sold by a vendor" as well.
Where: Database > Gear
Test: Open Filters, tick "Costs gold only" and "Sold by a vendor", and check that no Crown Shop items show.
