Changed: Ultimus Spectator only needs the Minimap add-on downloaded
Ultimus Spectator works as long as the Minimap add-on is downloaded. Your own Minimap no longer has to be on the overlay, and ticking Ultimus Spectator no longer puts your Minimap on.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Take your Minimap off the overlay (keep the add-on downloaded), tick Ultimus Spectator, and check Ultimus's map, quest and spells still show in the game.
