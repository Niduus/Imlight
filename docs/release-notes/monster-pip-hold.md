Fixed: Monsters saved up pips and then cast a weak spell
A monster could wait for its own big attack while that attack was still on cooldown, saving up pips for nothing, and then spend them on something small. The Sea Lord in Dragonspyre saved 7 pips and then cast Heck Hound. Now a monster only waits for a spell it can actually cast once it has the pips, so it spends saved pips on its school's best hit, for every monster in every world.
Where: In battle against monsters.
Test: Fight the Sea Lord in Dragonspyre. Once he has saved pips he spends them on a big spell of his own school, not on a small one.
