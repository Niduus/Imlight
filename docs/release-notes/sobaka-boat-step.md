Fixed: Sobaka's boat now counts in Zafaria
Fastest Hunk of Junk, Down By The River and Catching Shadows each ask you to use Sobaka's boat, but the game files never put that boat anywhere, so the step could not be done. The boat you can press at the Stone Town docks, and the one in Waterfront, now count for the step when you ride it. This only applies to a step that has nothing of its own to press in its area, so other quests keep their own objects.
Where: Zafaria, Stone Town docks and Waterfront.
Test: take Fastest Hunk of Junk from Sobaka in Stone Town, press the boat at the docks, and check that the step counts and you arrive in Waterfront to talk to Sobaka.
