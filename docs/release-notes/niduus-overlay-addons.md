New: Download Niduus's overlay add-ons
The overlay's Add-ons part now lists the add-ons Niduus made, straight from GitHub, each with a Download button (and "Download all"). The first one is the Minimap, in three shapes, plus a gold map frame style. When Niduus puts up a newer version, the button says "Get the newer one".
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Open Play > Overlay, scroll to Add-ons, click Download next to Minimap, then click "+ Minimap" under Your add-ons and check the map shows on the picture.
