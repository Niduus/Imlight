Fixed: Vladimir Darkflame showing up in boss form too early
In the Labyrinth, a second Vladimir Darkflame in his boss look stood next to the real one before you could talk to him. It was a placeholder from the game's zone editor that the live game never shows. Placeholders like it are now left out everywhere, except the few that a quest really talks to.
Where: Dragonspyre, the Labyrinth tower where you meet Vladimir, and other places with editor placeholders.
Test: enter Vladimir's tower in the Labyrinth on his quest; only the Vladimir you can talk to is there, and his boss form comes when the quest calls for the fight.
