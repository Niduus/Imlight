Fixed: Emperor's Retreat teleporter to the Keymaster's tower now works
After beating the Overseer in the Emperor's Retreat, the glowing teleporter that leads up to the Keymaster's tower did nothing, so players walked into it and stayed put with no way forward. It now takes you into the tower, and the tower's exit brings you back next to it. The rest of the dungeon after the tower (the obelisk combination and the door to Krokhotep) already works on the server.
Where: Krokotopia, Krokosphinx, Emperor's Retreat.
Test: Beat the Overseer, step into the teleporter by the Keymaster's door, beat the Colossus in the tower, read the tablets, then walk out. Set the six obelisks to match the tablets and the door to Krokhotep should open.
