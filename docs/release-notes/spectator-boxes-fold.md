Fixed: Spectator boxes can be folded small
The quest box and the spells box of the Ultimus Spectator, the Quest Team Spectator and Spectate Anyone can now be folded down so they take far less of the screen. Click the top line of a box, the one with the name, to fold it to that line plus the quest being played, or for the spells box the pips and the spell cast. Click it again to open it. Each box remembers whether it was folded the next time the game starts.
Where: the spectator boxes over the game.
Test: watch Ultimus with the Ultimus Spectator, click his name at the top of the quest box to fold it and again to open it, then do the same on the spells box during one of his battles.
