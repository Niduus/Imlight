New: A page for each TCO7 member
Each TCO7 member has its own page under TCO7, the same as Ultimus's page, with its own Issues and Logs. Everything on it is that member's own: what it is doing, its stats, gear, deck and quest, its diary and its issues. Its settings start the same as Ultimus's and can be changed for that member alone. The TCO7 page also shows what they last read from Ultimus.
Where: TCO7 > Tulkar
Test: Open Tulkar under TCO7 and use the Overview, Issues and Logs buttons at the top. Then open the Ultimus page: it still shows Ultimus.
