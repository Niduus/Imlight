Fixed: Encounters start when you walk into their spot
Some quests are encounters that start on their own when you step into a place, instead of being handed out by an NPC. The server never started them, so they could not be done. Now walking into the spot starts the encounter, unless you already did it.
Where: every world that has encounters, such as Empyrea.
Test: walk into the spot of an encounter you have not done and check that it shows up in your quest book.
