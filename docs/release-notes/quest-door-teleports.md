Fixed: The first Marleybone quest sends you to the right Royal Museum
With "Open all doors" on, the museum door in Regent's Square always picked its everyday exit, so A Mystery in Marleybone sent you to a museum where the quest could not go on. When a door has a quest exit and an everyday exit, the one that fits your quests now wins.
Test: Start Marleybone (talk to the dog at Wolfminster Abbey), walk into the Royal Museum door in Regent's Square, and check the quest goes on inside.

Fixed: Big Ben's elevators reach every floor and Meowiarty's room
The game rebuilt Big Ben with 20 floors and a boss room, but the server still had the old tower's floor map, so the first elevator did nothing and later ones dropped you on the wrong floor. Every elevator up and down, the boss room door and the way out after the boss now go where they should.
Test: Go into Big Ben, win each floor's fight, step into each elevator and check you come out on the next floor, all the way up to Meowiarty.

Fixed: The MooShu Water Dojo no longer sends you to Shoshun Village
The Water Dojo door in Jade Palace overlaps the gate to Shoshun Village. While the game loaded the dojo, the gate took you away instead. A second door now waits until the first door's loading is done.
Test: With the MooShu quest that sends you into the Water Dojo, walk into the dojo door in Jade Palace and check you land inside the dojo.

New: .stuck go takes you back to your quest
If a door or teleport ever leaves you in the wrong place, type .stuck go in chat and you are taken to where your quest's current step is (a main quest first).
Test: Type .stuck go while on a quest whose step is in another area, and check you are taken there.
