Fixed: The Young Battledrake flies you back to The Great Spyre after a lost fight with Malistaire Drake
If you lost to Malistaire Drake, or your Dungeon Recall ran out over a restart, walking up to the Young Battledrake did nothing, so there was no way back to The Great Spyre. Walking up to the drake on the Academy's top floor now flies you to Dragon's Roost while you are still on the step to defeat Malistaire Drake, the same as on the step to go to The Great Spyre.
Where: Dragonspyre Academy, the Young Battledrake on the top floor, during The Final Countdown.
Test: on The Final Countdown's step to defeat Malistaire Drake, stand in the Academy and walk up to the Young Battledrake; check that the dragon flies you to Dragon's Roost.
