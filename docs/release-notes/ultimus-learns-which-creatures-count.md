New: Ultimus learns which creatures count for a step
After each battle he wins for a fight or collect step, Ultimus writes down whether the step went up. He then goes for the creatures that counted first and leaves the ones that never did for last, even when the quest's own creature list matches nothing nearby. What he learned stays after a restart, and shows in his quest tracker.
Where: Ultimus, saved in drop-memory.json in his log folder.
