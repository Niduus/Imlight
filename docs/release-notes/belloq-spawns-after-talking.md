Fixed: Belloq did not come out to fight in his tent until you left and came back
In Zafaria's Under the Big Tent, Belloq only came out to fight if you were already on the "defeat Belloq" step when you walked into his tent. That step starts after you talk to him inside, so he never came until you went out and back in. Now an area looks again at what it brings out on arrival each time your quest moves on a step while you are in it, so Belloq comes out right after you talk to him, and other quests built the same way work too.
Where: Belloq's Tent in Zafaria, and any area whose quest creatures or happenings start as you walk in.
Test: on Under the Big Tent, walk into Belloq's Tent, talk to Belloq and check that the fight with him starts without leaving the tent.
