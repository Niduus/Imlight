Fixed: Kiosk pet owners are told when someone hatches with their pet
When another player hatches with a pet you put up at the kiosk, the game now shows its own "your pet has hatched with" message and gives you a Hatch Pepper. If you were offline, you get both the next time you use a kiosk. How many peppers you get is the Hatch Peppers setting.
Where: Any Pet Hatching Kiosk. Setting: Ahziael, Shops and Codes, Pet Hatching Kiosk bot, Hatch Peppers per hatch.
Test: Have another player hatch with your kiosk pet while you are online. The message shows and a Hatch Pepper is in your reagents.
