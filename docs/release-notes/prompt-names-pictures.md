Fixed: Missing names and pictures on Press X boxes
Some things you press X on had a blank name or no picture, like Milaka Jewelbender's crystal, the Crystal Samples and Flax. They now show the name and picture the game's own files give them. Teleport stones whose place gives them a name of their own now say it in the Press X box too, like "To Chronius' Tower", "To Spider Hive", "To Spiderkeeper's Tower" and "To Teleport Hub" in the Crystal Grove.
Where: The Crystal Grove, and anywhere else a Press X box was blank.
Test: walk up to Milaka's crystal, a Crystal Sample and a Flax plant, and to the Crystal Grove's teleport stones; each box shows a name and picture.
