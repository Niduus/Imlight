On the spell cards Ahziael draws, the school icon and the type icon now sit in the middle of their sockets, and the pip cost sits in the middle of its triangle. Where: Ahziael, every place that shows spell cards (bot decks, spell search, Lookup).
Test: Open a bot's deck and look at a card such as Great Tri-Roga or a shadow spell: the pips sit beside the number and you can tell which school they are.
