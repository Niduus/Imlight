New: Beastmoon Hunt Monster Mayhem
Beastmoon Hunt's monster mode now works. Join from the event window, pick a beast at the statues, buy battle cards with Battle Coins, take the rings and beat the monsters and the boss before the clock runs out. Beast levels and tiers are raised at the Beastmoon Upgrades helper with Hunter's Moonstones and Lunari, and the rewards and prices can be changed in the Beastmoon settings.
Where: the Beastmoon pavilion and its arenas.
Test: join a match alone from the event window, take a beast form, win a few battles, open a Coin Clam, and check the end panel shows your points.
