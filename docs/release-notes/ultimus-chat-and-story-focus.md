# Ultimus talks sensibly and sticks to the main story

Fixed: Ultimus's chat answers make sense now. He tracks what you just asked, so follow-ups work. He never answers a question with filler like "Ha, true!" or "Interesting!", and he never says anything untrue about himself.
- "what step" (or "no, the step of the quest") gets his real step from his quest book, with the count, e.g. "Defeat 3 Nirini Warrior (1 of 3)".
- "Can I fish next to you?" gets "Sure, go ahead!".
- "Wanna do a dungeon?", "Follow me" and duel or hatching invites get a kind no (he quests solo).
- "You're not allowed to hatch!" gets "Don't worry, I never hatch, train pets or craft."
- "Are you hurt?" or "Why are you standing still?" gets what he's really doing and his health.
- A question he can't place gets an honest "Not sure what you mean, sorry! I'm working on "Payback" right now."
- "ok", "brb" and similar get no answer.
- Asking the same question again gets a fuller answer, not a mute.
- Game words like "trash mobs" or "this quest is dumb" no longer count as rude.
- "nice weather" no longer gets "thank you".
Fixed: His typed answers always go out as typed chat, so lines like "Right now: ..." with missing words are gone. When you use Menu Chat, he answers in Menu Chat only when a phrase says exactly what he means ("Hello", "No thanks"). Random phrases like "Hello?" or "Sorry, I am hatching with someone else" are never picked. Otherwise he types his answer.
Fixed: No more double hello (a "Hello" before his real answer) when a player he met before talks to him.
Changed: Ultimus never farms creatures for XP. The main story comes first; he walks round creatures his step doesn't need. Only if he loses the same quest fight more than once, or a story quest needs a higher level, he does a side quest of his level given nearby. If there is none, he says so in his log (pink).
Fixed: He no longer goes off selling gear while hurt or to a far shop. He only sells when his backpack is nearly full and a shop is close by, and stops trying if a sale didn't free any space.
Fixed: A restart no longer counts as "a new world", so he doesn't go shopping mid-quest after every restart. Shops that turned out not to sell an item are remembered, so no more repeat trips for the Jewel of Instinct.
Fixed: His goals line and progress check read his real quest, even while he heals, shops or travels. The first progress check after a restart no longer says "yes" by default.
