New: Quest Team, four wizards that test the story for you
Four wizards (Ice, Life, Death and Storm) play the story on your server by themselves, always together and in that order. They start at level 1 in Wizard City and play like players: they walk everywhere, talk to quest givers, fight as a team (Ice tanks, Life heals, Death backs up, Storm hits) and walk to the Ravenwood trainers to learn their spells (Ice also learns Feint, Life also learns Tower Shield). Off by default. They never touch your own wizards.
Where: Quest Team > Quest Team
Test: Start the server, turn the Quest Team on, and watch the four wizards log in and start questing near the start of Wizard City. Type .tp to their street to see them.

New: Quest Team issues and logs
When a quest does not work, when the team is stuck, when it loses the same fight too often, or when a quest takes much longer than expected, a banner at the top of Ahziael says "An issue with the Quest Team has occurred". Team Issues shows each one with the team's log from when it happened, and Team Logs shows every quest they started and finished with how long it took.
Where: Quest Team > Team Issues
Test: Let the team play for a while, then open Team Issues and Team Logs. When the banner shows, click Show what happened.
