Fixed: Wisps could be picked up during a battle
While you were in a battle, a health, mana or gold wisp floating near your circle could still be picked up. Now nobody in a battle (against creatures or another player) can pick up a wisp. Once the battle is over, wisps work as before, including the ones the battle drops.
Where: In the game, any area with wisps
Test: Find a wisp near some creatures, start a battle right next to it, and stay in the battle a few rounds. The wisp stays where it is and your health, mana and gold do not change. Win the battle, walk over the wisp, and it is picked up.
