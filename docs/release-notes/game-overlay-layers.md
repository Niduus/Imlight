Changed: The Ahziael line is permanent
"Ahziael made by Niduus | version" always shows on the game, in full, with the look Ahziael gives it. It can't be turned off, hidden, restyled, made see-through or covered; you can only move it (pick its corner or side and how far in). The "Show my texts and boxes" switch now hides only what you made.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Pick "Ahziael line" in the list: only where it sits can change. Turn off "Show my texts and boxes": the line and the sparkle button stay on the game.

New: Layers and spacing for overlay texts and boxes
Each text and box has a Layer (higher is drawn on top) and a Spacing. With Spacing above 0, one that laps over another is moved off it, leaving that much room between them; with 0 it may lap over (like words on a box). Nothing can lap over the Ahziael line: anything that would is moved off it.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Add two boxes in the same place, give the second a Spacing of 20, and check it moves off the first with a gap. Move a box onto the Ahziael line and check it is pushed off.

Changed: Nothing is put on your desktop
The overlay on its own starts only from inside Ahziael (Play > Overlay, "Start the overlay on its own"), and saved overlay designs go in the "Overlay designs" folder of Ahziael's folder.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Click "Save design to a file" and check it opens in the Overlay designs folder.
