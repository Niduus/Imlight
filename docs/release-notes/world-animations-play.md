Fixed: World animations play when a quest changes something
When a quest or the area changes an object, it now plays its animation first, the way the real game does. The Dark Cave bubbles pop, Bartleby's mouth swings open, and gates, doors, cages and obelisks open or light up instead of jumping straight to their new look. A switch turns it off if you ever want the old way.
Where: Game Rules > Battles and World
Test: On a new wizard, go to Dark Cave in Cyclops Lane for "Cave Escape" (the bubbles step) and click a bubble: it should pop with a sound. Or finish the Bartleby quest in Ravenwood and watch his mouth open.
