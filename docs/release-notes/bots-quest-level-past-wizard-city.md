Fixed: Ultimus no longer thinks quests past Wizard City are level 1
Many quests in later worlds carry level 1 in the game's files, so Ultimus kept saying "my level is fine for this level 1 quest" in Marleybone and judged his fights and side quests by it. Now such a quest counts as the level its world starts at, so his checks, his fights and his level-ups match the world he is in.
