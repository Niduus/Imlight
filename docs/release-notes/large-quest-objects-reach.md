Fixed: The fallen Watch Tower in Zafaria can be repaired
In the Zafaria quest In The Cold Distance the fallen Watch Tower never offered "Repair", so players could not finish the step. The tower is very long and the server only offered its button within a short distance of its foot. Large quest objects like this one now offer their button when you stand next to any part of them.
Where: Zafaria, Savannah, and any other large quest object. No setting.
Test: Take In The Cold Distance, walk up to the side of the fallen Watch Tower, and press X to repair it. The step should finish and the arrow should move on to Captain Bheki Blackhoof.
