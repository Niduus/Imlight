New: the Quest Team writes down where they stood at a door that didn't work

When a walk-through door doesn't take the Quest Team after 8 tries, they now write each try into exit-tries.txt in the Quest Team's logs folder. Each try shows where the leader stood and where they walked out and back in, the same way Ultimus does. Then they keep trying on their own.

Where: Quest Team, exit-tries.txt in its logs folder, also behind Ahziael's red door note on the Ultimus page.
Test: If the Quest Team log says a door never took them, open exit-tries.txt in the Quest Team folder. It should list each try.
