New: The Calling of 7 (TCO7)
Seven new wizards play the game by themselves, one of each school: Tulkar (Ice), Cyndr (Fire), Kurrent (Storm), Mythis (Myth), Idunn (Life), Garm (Death) and Ra (Balance), all with the Essential title. Each one is its own Ultimus, with the same brain and the same rules, so they quest like a player and never skip anything. They read everything Ultimus has learned, add what they learn to it, and try to beat his time on every quest. They are off until you turn them on.
Where: TCO7 > TCO7
Test: Start the server, turn on the TCO7 switch and wait a minute. Each member shows as playing on the TCO7 page, and in the game you can find them with ".ultimus tp Tulkar" (or any other name).
