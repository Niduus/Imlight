Fixed: Dungeon sigils lead to the right room
Some sigils sent players to the wrong room because the server guessed where they lead from their names. The underground sigil in District of the Stars went to the wrong cave, so Teeth of the Shark could never reach Brutus Starkwave. Sigils now go where the game's own zone file says, and seven wrong rooms in Avalon, Azteca, Celestia, Dragonspyre, Empyrea and Khrysalis were corrected, along with the spot you land on in 56 more.
Where: dungeon sigils in every world.
Test: in Celestia, District of the Stars, step on the underground sigil; you land in Starkwave's Lair. Teeth of the Shark steps 3 and 4 can now be finished.
