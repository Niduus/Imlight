New: Zoom the minimap in the game
The minimap now has + and - buttons on its corner, next to the full screen button. How close you leave it is kept on your PC. You can pick how much one press zooms, the closest and farthest it can go, and a quick zoom preset (Very close to Very far). The same limits apply to the full screen map.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Click + and - on the minimap in the game, close the game, open it again. The zoom should be as you left it.

New: More minimap extras
New choices: a soft wedge the way you face, grid lines (with how far apart and their colour), a thin line round the edge of the ground you can walk on, and names in the same colour as their icons.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Pick the minimap in the designer, open "Minimap extras", turn them on and look at the map in the game.
