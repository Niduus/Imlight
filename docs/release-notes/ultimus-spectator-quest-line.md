Fixed: Ultimus Spectator draws the line to Ultimus's quest
Ultimus's own minimap now draws the line to his quest the same way your Minimap does for the quest you pick in the Quest Tracker: from where he stands, along the streets, through teleporters and doors, to his step (or to the exit toward the step's area). When the step has no spot on the map, the line goes to the spot Ultimus himself is heading to. It uses your Quest Tracker's line colours.
Where: Play > Overlay
Test: Put Ultimus Spectator on with the Minimap, turn Ultimus on, and watch the purple Ultimus map: a dashed line runs from him to his quest step.
