New: Ultimus buys a few treasure cards and draws them in tough fights

Ultimus now keeps a few treasure cards of his own school, two strong hits and a heal that fit his level. He buys them only from a treasure card vendor in the area he is in, never from the Bazaar, and he never spends his gold reserve on them. He puts them in his deck the same way a player does, and in a tough fight (a boss, three or more enemies, low health, or enemies that hit hard) he presses Draw once a round and plays the card when it fits.

Where: Ultimus, out in the world and in battle.
Test: Give Ultimus some spare gold and let him walk past a treasure card vendor, then watch his Thoughts for the buy and his deck for the cards. Start a boss fight with him and see him draw a treasure card.
