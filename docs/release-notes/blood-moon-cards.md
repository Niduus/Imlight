Added: Blood Moon cards
Cards made in Blood Moon's Make window now work in battle. The game shows each one as the game card it was set to look like, while the server casts it with its own pips, accuracy, school and effects. A card comes into battle as an item card of a Blood Moon gear piece that holds it. Type .customcards to list them.
Where: Blood Moon, Make, Cards and Gear; in the game, .customcards.
Test: make a card that looks like Fire Cat and deals 900 Fire damage, put it on a Blood Moon wand, wear the wand, start a fight and cast the card; the creature takes about 900 damage.
