New: Ultimus keeps tips players give him while he is stuck
When a player tells Ultimus something that sounds like advice (for example "go through the door on the left" or "talk to Khaba first") while he is stuck or has set a step aside, he thanks them and writes the tip on that quest. Tips show under the quest in the Broken quests list and in the Needs help box on the Ultimus page. Private details are blocked as always.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus Issues, Broken quests; Ultimus page, Needs help.
Test: While Ultimus is stuck, whisper him a tip such as "try the door behind you". He should answer "Thanks for the tip!" and the tip should show under that quest.
