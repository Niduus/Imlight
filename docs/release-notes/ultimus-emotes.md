New: Ultimus waves, cheers and sits
Ultimus now uses emotes like a player. He waves at a player who says hello near him, cheers after a tough fight he won, and sits down when he has had nothing to do for a minute. He learns the emote names from players' own emote wheels, so if you don't see one yet, play that emote once near anyone.
