Fixed: The Devourer no longer keeps you devoured forever
Alone against The Devourer, you were let go and devoured again in the same moment, so you never got a turn back and the battle waited between turns. Now a devoured wizard passes right away, comes back after 3 rounds, and the one just let go is never the next one devoured. Alone, you get 3 rounds back before the next Devour.
Test: Fight The Devourer in the Catacombs alone. After the Devour at the end of round 1, rounds 2 and 3 should go by quickly, and you should be back to play at round 5.

Fixed: Renegade Druid's Intercept comes from him, not his Druids
At the start of round 2 Renegade Druid now puts the Intercept (Shadow Sentinel) on each of his three Druids himself, so they take the hits meant for him. Before, the Druids cast it themselves.
Test: Fight Renegade Druid and watch round 2: he casts it, and a hit on him lands on one of his Druids.

Fixed: The way to Sir and Squire Greggor is open
In the Foetid Crypt, crystal walls after the first and second rooms only open for a crypt quest the server doesn't have, so no one could reach the Greggors. The walls are gone now; the room fights are still on the way.
Test: Enter the Foetid Crypt (No Place Like Catacomb) and walk through all three rooms to Sir Greggor.

Changed: Late creatures fight with late spells
Creatures whose deck only the real game's servers have used to get an old Azteca-era school deck, or only six starter spells for Shadow, Star and Sun creatures (The Devourer, King Detritus and others). Shadow, Star and Sun creatures now play the Death, Balance and Fire decks, and creatures from rank 14 on also get their school's level 100 to 130 spells (Rusalka's Wrath, Iron Sultan, the Scion spells and so on), so Renegade Druid no longer plays the plain Rank 18 Storm deck.
Test: Fight a few Catacombs creatures and check they cast late spells, not only the starter ones.
