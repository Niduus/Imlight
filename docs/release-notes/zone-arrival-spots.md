Fixed: Arriving in a new area puts you at the right entrance
Some ways into an area did not say where to put you, so you landed at the area's default start spot, sometimes far from where you came in. You now arrive at the entrance that faces the area you just left, as the game's own area files mark it.
Where: area changes in every world, starting with Mirage.
