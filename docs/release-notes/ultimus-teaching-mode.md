Fixed: Ultimus can walk a player through a quest step by step
Say "walk me through this quest" (or name the quest) near Ultimus and he tells you its step and where it is, with the way there when it is in another area. He then waits for you and tells you the next step each time yours changes, until the quest is done or you say "got it" or "stop". His own story waits meanwhile. It can be turned off with the Teaching switch on the Ultimus page.
Where: Ultimus's chat and his log ("TEACHING:" lines).
Test: stand by Ultimus, say "walk me through this quest", finish a step and check he tells you the next one.
