Fixed: Magic: The Archmastering never let you into the Arena

After you talk to Diego the Duelmaster in "Magic: The Archmastering" (the Wizard City quest after Dragonspyre that leads on to Celestia), the next step asks you to enter the Arena, which for this quest is the Archmastery practice arena. Nothing on the server led there, so the Arena door in Unicorn Way always took you to the normal Duel Arena and the step never finished. Now, while you are on that step, the Arena door next to Diego takes you into the Archmastery arena, and its doorways bring you back out in front of the Arena door so you can talk to Diego again. The bots now see that way too.

Where: Unicorn Way, the Arena door beside Diego the Duelmaster.

Test: on "Magic: The Archmastering" after talking to Diego, walk into the Arena door; you should land in the Archmastery arena and the step should finish. Walk out through either doorway and talk to Diego to finish the quest.
