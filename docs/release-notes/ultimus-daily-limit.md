New: A daily play limit for Ultimus
You can now set the most hours Ultimus plays in a day. When he reaches it, he rests somewhere safe until the next day and writes it in his diary. 0 means no limit, which is the default.
Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page, Most hours he plays a day.
Test: Set it to 1, let him play for an hour, and his status should say he is resting for the day.
