Added: Pick who gets each player cheat
Every player cheat now has a "Who gets this cheat" box at the top of its page. Pick only you on this PC, every wizard on the server, the wizards you tick, or every wizard on the accounts you tick. Each of them gets the cheat in their own battles on any PC, so you no longer need to be in the fight. Above the list, "Show cheats for" shows only the cheats one wizard gets, and a new cheat made while a wizard is shown is for that wizard.
Where: Player Cheats page in Ahziael.
Test: Make a cheat, pick "Only the wizards I tick" and tick a friend's wizard, then Save. Let the friend fight without you: the cheat goes off for them and not for you.
