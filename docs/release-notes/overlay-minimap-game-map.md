# Minimap: the game's own map page under the map

New: the minimap can show the game's own hand-drawn map page of the area (the same picture as the map in your spellbook) under the streets and icons. It turns and zooms with the minimap. Ahziael reads the page from your game files and never changes them.
The pages are drawn by hand, so they line up only roughly with the real streets: usually within a few steps. Where the game's own pin points for a page are missing or out of date (Unicorn Way), Ahziael lines the page up by the area's shape instead, which is rougher. Areas without a map page just don't show one.

Where: Play > Overlay > click your Minimap > "The game's own map page (lines up roughly)": tick Show, and use the slider for how see-through it is. You may want to lower or turn off "Streets, grass, water and walls" when the page is on.

Test:
1. Update Ahziael.
2. In Play > Overlay, click the Minimap, tick Show next to "The game's own map page", and save.
3. Click Play, log in, and walk around Unicorn Way, the Commons, Ravenwood and Triton Avenue.
4. The game's map page should show under your arrow and turn with you. Tell me any area where it is far off.
