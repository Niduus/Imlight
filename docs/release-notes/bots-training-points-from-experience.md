New: Ultimus and the Quest Team spend training points on what their fights show they need
Ultimus and each Quest Team wizard now keep a record of their own fights: how low their health went, the biggest hit they took in one round, how long the fight lasted, and whether they won. From their latest fights they pick what they need most, a heal when fights leave them low, a strong shield when big hits land, or a way to hit harder when fights drag on. They then plan the whole chain of spells the trainers ask for before it, for example Imp, Leprechaun and Legend Shield before Sprite, and save their points for it. They say the plan and the reason in their thoughts and diary, and change it when their fights change. A trip to the trainer is made for a planned spell even outside Wizard City.
Where: Ultimus and the Quest Team. Fights are kept in logs/bots/training-experience.json, and Ultimus's plan is also in his training-plan.txt.
Test: Let Ultimus fight a few battles, then read his thoughts or training-plan.txt for "Training points" and the spells still to learn. He should go train them when he can afford them.
