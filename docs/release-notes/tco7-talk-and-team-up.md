New: The TCO7 talk to each other and team up
When two TCO7 members meet, they say hello and ask each other which quest they are on, out loud in game chat. When both are free and on the same quest, one asks the other to team up, and they quest together until their quests split, then each goes on alone. Ultimus himself never joins them, so his records stay his own.
Where: TCO7 > TCO7
Test: Turn the TCO7 on and go where two of them are questing in the same area. You see them greet each other, and two on the same quest group up after a short chat.
