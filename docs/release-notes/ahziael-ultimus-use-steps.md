New: Ahziael shows the quest steps where Ultimus may need to search

On Ahziael's Ultimus page, the Progress box now shows the top of Ultimus's check of the story's "click these things" steps under his progress. These are the steps where the things only appear later, so you can see where he may slow down without opening any files. The full list is also in "Copy all logs".

Where: Ahziael, Ultimus page, Progress box.
