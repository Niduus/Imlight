Fixed: The Dismiss button on your minion works
Clicking Dismiss on your own minion in a battle now sends it away. In the middle of planning it leaves right away; if you click it while the spells are playing, it leaves once the round is over. It does not use up your turn.
Test: Cast a minion (for example Animate or Fire Elemental), then click the minion and press Dismiss. It should leave the battle.

Changed: A new minion replaces your old one
Casting a minion card while you already have a minion no longer makes you pass. Your old minion leaves and the new one takes its circle. You can turn this off with "One minion per wizard at a time" to keep both when there is room.
Where: Game Rules > Battles and World
Test: Cast a minion, then on the next round cast a different minion card. The old one should leave and the new one should stand in its place.
