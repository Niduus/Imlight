Added: A TCO7 squad that groups up for good and catches up together
Up to four TCO7 members can be set as a squad. The squad groups up and stays grouped: the one furthest behind in the story leads and the others come along and help it catch up, then they quest on together and no longer split when a quest branches, like at the Three Streets. Ultimus still quests alone and the other members go at their own pace.
Where: Ahziael, TCO7 page, the Squad box, or .ultimus squad Tulkar Cyndr Kurrent Mythis in the game.
Test: Type four names in the Squad box, wait a minute, and check that they are in one group and stay in it after the next quest.
