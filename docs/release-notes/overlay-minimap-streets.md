New: The minimap shows the streets
The minimap now draws the area you are in from above, under its icons: streets and paths, grass, water, and walls and fences. They come from the game's own area files, so every area has its own shape. They turn and zoom with the map. You can turn them off, make them see-through, or pick each one's colour.
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it
Test: Add or pick your minimap, then walk around Unicorn Way. The street shape should match where you can walk, and you should stay on the streets as you move. If the streets look flipped left to right, tick "Swap left and right".
