Fixed: Ultimus and the Quest Team find the things a quest asks them to click

Some quest steps ask you to click several things spread around an area, like the four gems in the Hall of Champions for "Get Some Bling". The quest arrow only points at one spot for these, so Ultimus kept walking around the hall and back out again without ever finding a gem. Now he and the Quest Team look for the real things the step needs in that area and click them one after another, like a player would.

Where: Ultimus and the Quest Team, any "click these things" quest step (Krokotopia, Hall of Champions, "Get Some Bling").
Test: Let Ultimus reach "Get Some Bling" in the Hall of Champions. He should walk to each gem and click it, then talk to General Khaba.
