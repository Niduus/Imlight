Fixed: Ship skip sign always offers its "press X"
The skip sign on the Krokotopia flying ship only offered its "press X" once you took a step, so standing still on deck left you with no way to skip the ride. Anything right next to where you arrive in a zone now offers its "press X" straight away, without moving first. This covers both Krokotopia ships, the Marleybone airship signs, and any door, sign or person beside an arrival spot.
Where: the Oasis and Krokosphinx flying ships in Krokotopia, Marleybone's airship flights, and every zone.
Test: board the ship at the Oasis and do not move; the sign's "press X" shows once the loading screen is gone, and using it takes you to Krokosphinx. Do the same on the ship back and on a Marleybone flight.
