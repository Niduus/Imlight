Fixed: Players could not find the commands that fix their own quests and take them to their quest
Every player could already type .stuck to put their quests right and .stuck go to be taken to the area of the step they are on, but .help only showed these to staff, so players thought there was no such command. They now show in .help for every player, together with .questbook and .skip.
Where: the game's chat, with .help quests.
Test: on a normal player account type .help quests and check that .stuck, .stuck go and .questbook are listed, then type .stuck go and check it takes you to your quest's area.
