New: Spectators show and track dungeon quests

The Ultimus and Quest Team Spectator quest boxes now show a Track line when the bot holds a dungeon (Instance) quest. The dungeon quest is tracked by itself: its steps show in the box and its way is drawn on the spectator map. Click Main on the Track line to follow the main quest instead, or click Instance to go back. When there is no dungeon quest, or it ends, the box goes back to the main quest by itself.

Where: The Ultimus Spectator and Quest Team Spectator quest boxes and maps over the game, and their boxes on Ahziael's Ultimus and Quest Team pages.
Test: When Ultimus or the Quest Team enters a dungeon, the box should say "Track (auto)" with the Instance lit and the map line going to the dungeon step. Click Main and the box and map should follow the main quest.
