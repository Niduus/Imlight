Fixed: Ultimus no longer cuts across streets through creature paths
When Ultimus could see where he was going, he sometimes walked straight there across the street, right through the paths creatures walk, and got pulled into fights his quest didn't need. Now his shortcuts keep clear of creature paths as well as the creatures standing nearby, and a remembered walk that passes through a creature path is not reused. When a creature he doesn't need walks across his way just ahead and there is no way round it, he waits up to 8 seconds for it to move on, like a player timing a run between patrols. If it still blocks the way, he goes and takes the fight. Creatures his quest needs, and fights when he is under the level his story needs, are walked to as before.
Where: Ultimus everywhere; the Quest Team and Bot Players also skip remembered walks that pass through creature paths.
