New: A detailed minimap on the game overlay
Add a Minimap to your overlay and it shows what is around your wizard, live: a gold star for main quest steps, a blue diamond for side quest steps, a ! over NPCs with a quest for you, red dots for mobs (with a ring when a quest needs them), a skull for bosses, sigils, doors, chests, and other wizards and teammates. Quest steps that are off the map show as an arrow on its edge, and the quests you hold are listed under it, with the place to go when a step is somewhere else. It can turn with you or keep north up, be round or square, and zoom in or out. Each kind can be turned off or given its own colour, and every change shows right away.
Where: Play > Overlay > Design it
Test: Open the designer, click Add minimap, and look at the made-up map in the picture. Then start the game with Play, log in, and walk around Wizard City: your quest step, mobs and the place name should show and move as you walk. If things on your left show on the right, tick "Swap left and right".
