New: The Quest Team sees the tips players gave Ultimus
Tips players give Ultimus while he is stuck are now kept by quest and shared with the Quest Team. When the team gets stuck on that quest, its log shows what players told Ultimus, and any issue the team files about the quest lists the tips too. Marking a quest fixed clears its old tips.
Where: Ahziael, Quest Team log and Issues.
Test: Give Ultimus a tip while he is stuck on a quest, then let the Quest Team reach the same quest. If the team gets stuck there, its log should say "players told Ultimus about" that quest.
