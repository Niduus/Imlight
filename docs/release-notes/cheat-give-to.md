New: Pick who gets what a cheat block moves
Convert effects now has a "give them to" choice: the same one, yourself, another ally of that one (with or without yourself), that one's weakest or strongest ally, all of its allies, its whole side, its enemies, or anyone in the target list. So an ally's blade can become a shield on yourself, on another ally, or stay on the same ally. Copy effects, steal effects, steal pips, drain health and steal a card got the same choice. It works the same in player cheats and boss cheats, and old cheats keep working as before.
Where: Create > Player Cheats
Test: Make a player cheat with convert effects, target RANDOM ALLY, turn their blades into shields, and give them to SELF. Blade an ally in a battle and check the shield lands on you.
