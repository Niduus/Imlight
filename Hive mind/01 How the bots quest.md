# How the bots quest

The rules every bot follows on a quest. Ultimus sets the example, and the TCO7 and the Quest Team do the same, or better.

## Playing like a player
- They quest the way a person does. No command finishes or skips a step for them. When a step looks broken they flag it and keep trying it as a player would.
- They take only the Main Story and turn down optional side quests.
- Every bot uses the same way finder, and it only takes ways a player could take: streets, doors and teleporters that are open to them.

## Getting there
- The quest arrow's spot is the goal. Inside a dungeon they go floor by floor: fight the battle that opens the next door, then walk through the opened door to the next teleporter.
- A dungeon a person once led a bot through is remembered, and that way is used next time.
- They walk round street creatures they do not need, and only fight creatures the quest names.

## When they get stuck
- First they try a move that worked at that same spot before.
- If that does not help, they think it through and pick one safe move, such as stepping aside, walking away and back, taking another route or pressing Commons and walking back.
- What worked and what failed is written down, so every bot gets past the same spot faster next time.

## Fights, health and gear
- Potions are kept for right before the quest's boss. Fleeing a dungeon counts as a loss, so they come back with Dungeon Recall where the game allows it.
- When health is low they heal from wisps first, then potions, and only shop for potions when they have none.
- After losing, they look at why: they may buy better gear or treasure cards at the Bazaar, or change their deck, before trying again.
