# How the bots fight

Every questing bot's fights on Niduus's server (Ultimus, the TCO7, the Kitharas Family and the Quest Team; collected November 22, 2026), added up by kind of fight. Zoom and True Form are left out: their fights are not a questing bot's. "build up" is a blade or trap first, then one big hit. "hit now" is the best hit at once. "stack up" is every blade and trap first, then one big hit (used against foes stronger than the bot). A style shows only after 5 fights of that kind.

Ultimus fights bosses alone (Niduus's rule) and learns his own card mix: mostly hits in his main deck, support in his treasure cards.
