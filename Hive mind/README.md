# About the Hive mind

The Hive mind is what the bots know about getting the story done: Ultimus, the seven TCO7 wizards, the Quest Team and the questing street bots. Ahziael shows these pages on its Hive mind tab, and checks this folder on GitHub when it opens, when the server starts and every 30 minutes, so a page changed here shows in Ahziael without an update.

Everything here is meant to be certain. A quest is listed only when a bot finished it from start to finish by itself. A run is left out when Ahziael had to finish a step for it, when a step was flagged as broken, or when the bot picked the quest up partway or after a restart. A fix for getting stuck is listed only when it worked every time it was tried. A fight style is listed only after 5 fights of that kind.

## What is in this folder
- How the bots quest: the rules every bot follows on a quest.
- How the bots fight: which way of fighting wins for each kind of fight.
- Quests: one page per world, quest by quest, with who finished it, the best time, the steps and fights, and what got a bot unstuck there.

## Questing street bots
The questing street bots (up to 30, turned on with .streetbots in the game) play the story with Ultimus's brain and add to the same knowledge. They are there to find more than one way through: now and then, where a way past a stuck spot is already known, one of them tries a different safe move first. What worked and what failed are both written down, so every bot can pick the move with the best record and avoid the ones that keep failing.

## Your own server
The Hive mind tab also collects the same kind of facts from the bots on your own server, each time the server starts. That part stays on your computer.
