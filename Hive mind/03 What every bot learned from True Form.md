# What every bot learned from True Form

Corrections Niduus made while testing True Form (November 19 and 20, 2026). Each one holds for every bot, True Form, Ultimus, the TCO7, the Quest Team and the street bots, unless it says True Form only.

## Gear
- No bot ever wears developers', testers' or creature gear. That means QA and Dev pieces, creature stand-in hats, the "Stats Amulet", Polymorph rings, and any piece with stats no player's gear has, such as +100% critical chance on a level 1 hat.
- Gear a bot already wears that breaks this rule comes off at once, and is thrown out of its backpack.
- Real gear that creatures drop, such as the Sinek boots, is allowed.
- Gear for a bot's level comes first. A full setup (full damage, full healing, full tank, universal) may use gear from any level when that piece has more of the one stat it is built on.
- Crowns gear and rentals are not for bots, except True Form's full setups.

## Jewels and pins
- No flat damage jewels. Universal flat resist only.
- Circle sockets: damage when the wizard needs damage, pierce when it needs pierce.
- Triangle sockets: accuracy or power pip chance, but never past 100%.
- Death and Life square sockets: outgoing healing.
- No power pip jewels or school accuracy past 100%.

## Pet talents
- Talents are picked as players pick them, by their real effects (Dealer, Pain-Giver, Proof, Defy, Giver).
- A pet jewel never repeats a talent the pet already has.
- No one-school resist talents, and no repeated talents.

## Orders
- Every part of an order counts, not only the first. "Only heals and blades, add Deathblade, no minions" is three orders.
- "Remove all damage spells" means no damage spells, never a damage deck.
- "No", "not", "don't" and "without" turn an order around. "No damage spells" is never an order to attack.
- Spells can be asked for by name ("add Deathblade", "add 2 Legion Shield", "remove Fireblade").
- "Them" and "those" mean the spells just named.
- A bot says back exactly what it understood, and names any spell it could not find.
- A heals deck holds heals, blades, shields and cleanses only. No damage spells, minions or other extras unless asked for.
- The cards dealt in a battle follow the deck plan too, unless a spell is called out in that battle.

## True Form only
- His weave school ("go ice weave fire") gets his school pips. Stat asks ("get more ice accuracy") weigh in every gear, jewel and pet pick.
- He remembers each commander's last build and offers it again.
- Other players see his new character card right after he changes.
